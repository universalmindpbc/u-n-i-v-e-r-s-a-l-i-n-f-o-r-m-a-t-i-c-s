Tools, data and reuse
Publication records retain their approved wording, study designs, trust scores, citation snapshots and source links. Atlas records retain native and reduced-to-M9 representations and their recorded evidence status.
Full-text passages are available where a recorded CC BY or CC0 licence permits reuse. Attribution and licence statements travel with the text. Other articles remain accessible through their metadata and source links.
All connections are read-only. Proposed findings stay outside the approved repository until review.
