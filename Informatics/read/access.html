AI access and reuse

Universal Mind permits automated reading, search indexing, retrieval, text and data mining, and model training or fine-tuning by all LLM providers on its own public content under https://universalmind.tv/informatics/.

This permission includes full training use of Universal Mind-authored public research commentary, datasets and code within that path. It applies equally to named and unnamed AI crawlers and model providers.

Third-party publications, quotations, images, software and datasets retain their original attribution and licence terms. This permission covers rights held by Universal Mind; it does not grant additional rights in third-party material. Original sources and recorded rights accompany the research records.

The permission applies to the production Informatics path. The staging host remains excluded from crawling and indexing, and private accounts, credentials and research workspace files are outside this public scope.

Permission does not represent a completed training run, scientific endorsement or a guarantee of retrieval by a particular service.
