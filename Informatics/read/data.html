How to assess the work

Inspect each claim alongside its source, study design, measurement or mathematical calculation, limitations and revision. A mathematical pattern does not establish a biological mechanism. Computational and hardware experiments retain their specific test conditions. Source publication scores and platform credibility are separate assessments.
