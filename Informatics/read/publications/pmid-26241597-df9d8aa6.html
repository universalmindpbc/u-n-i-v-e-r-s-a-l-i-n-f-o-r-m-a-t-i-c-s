Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction for Posttraumatic Stress Disorder Among Veterans: A Randomized Clinical Trial

Polusny MA, Erbes CR, Thuras P, Moran A, Lamberty GJ, Collins RC, Rodman JL, Lim KO. · JAMA · 2015 · Randomized controlled trial; single-blind (clinical assessors)

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

MBSR produced greater improvement in self-reported PTSD symptoms than present-centred therapy. More participants achieved clinically significant symptom improvement, but loss of PTSD diagnosis did not differ significantly.

Recorded limitations

The mean advantage was modest, and treatment-session duration differed between groups. This study did not measure gene expression.
