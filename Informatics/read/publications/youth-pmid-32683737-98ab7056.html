A randomised controlled trial of a relationship-focussed mobile phone application for improving adolescents' mental health.

O'Dea B, Han J, Batterham PJ, Achilles MR, Calear AL, Werner-Seidler A, Parker B, Shand F, Christensen H. · Journal of child psychology and psychiatry, and allied disciplines · 2020 · Randomized controlled trial report · double blinding not verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Primary depression outcome did not differ. Wellbeing and help-seeking intentions improved; anxiety, social efficacy and social support did not.

Recorded limitations

Mostly girls, younger than target, self-report, inactive comparator; intentions are not actual help-seeking.
