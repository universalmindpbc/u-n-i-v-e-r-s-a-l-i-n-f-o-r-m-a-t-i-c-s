Stress and stress reactivity in posttraumatic stress disorder (PTSD) following eye movement desensitization (EMD): A randomized controlled trial.

Susanty E, Sijbrandij M, van der Mee DJ, Srisayekti W, Suparman Y, Huizink AC. · Journal of behavior therapy and experimental psychiatry · 2025 · Randomized controlled component trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Ninety-one adults with PTSD received eye-movement desensitization or memory retrieval alone. Both groups showed reduced stress reactivity; the study found no specific overall benefit of eye movements and inconsistent cortisol differences.

Recorded limitations

Saliva timing depended on participant adherence. This is the same registered component trial as the 2024 neurocognitive report, not an independent replication.
