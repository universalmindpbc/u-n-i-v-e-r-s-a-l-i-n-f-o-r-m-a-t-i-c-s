Effects of a Standardized Music Intervention on Propofol Requirements and Serum Interleukin-6 and Prolactin in Deeply Sedated Mechanically Ventilated Adults: A Prespecified Secondary Analysis of a Randomized Clinical Trial

Fernández-Álvarez C, Mosteiro-Díaz MP, Zuazua-Rico D, Maestro-González A. · Healthcare (Basel, Switzerland) · 2026 · Open-label randomized trial; secondary analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The trial found no significant adjusted between-group difference in serum IL-6 or prolactin. Propofol requirements decreased more in the music group among patients receiving that drug.

Recorded limitations

The parent trial was powered for a different primary outcome. An IL-6 protein measurement is separate from IL6 gene expression.
