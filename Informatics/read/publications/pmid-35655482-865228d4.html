Effects of the Alpha, Beta, and Gamma Binaural Beat Brain Stimulation and Short-Term Training on Simultaneously Assessed Visuospatial and Verbal Working Memories, Signal Detection Measures, Response Times, and Intrasubject Response Time Variabilities: A Within-Subject Randomized Placebo-Controlled Clinical Trial.

Rakhshan V, Hassani-Abharian P, Joghataei M, Nasehi M, Khosrowabadi R. · BioMed research international · 2022 · Randomized placebo-controlled crossover experiment

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 31 healthy participants, brief 10 Hz binaural stimulation improved selected visuospatial response-speed and stability measures during a working-memory task.

Recorded limitations

Effects were small and differed across sensory modalities; auditory-verbal measures did not show the same pattern. There were 155 condition sessions, not 155 independent participants, and no clinical outcome was tested.
