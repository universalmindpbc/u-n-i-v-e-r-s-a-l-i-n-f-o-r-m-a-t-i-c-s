Epigenetic impact of a 1-week intensive multimodal group program for adolescents with multiple adverse childhood experiences.

Kaliman P, Cosín-Tomás M, Madrid A, Roque López S, Llanez-Anaya E, Papale LA, Alisch RS, Davidson RJ. · Scientific Reports · 2022 · Randomized trial · molecular analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A one-week programme combining mindfulness, artistic expression and EMDR group therapy studied 44 adolescent girls with multiple adverse experiences. Forty-nine DNA-methylation loci differed with intervention; SIRT5 and TRAPPC2L changes correlated with trauma measures.

Recorded limitations

The programme has several components. Saliva methylation findings do not isolate EMDR; the study detected no intervention effect on intrinsic epigenetic age acceleration.
