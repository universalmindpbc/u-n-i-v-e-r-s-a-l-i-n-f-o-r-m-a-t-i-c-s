A mindfulness-based intervention to increase resilience to stress in university students (the Mindful Student Study): a pragmatic randomised controlled trial.

Galante J, Dufour G, Vainre M, Wagner AP, Stochl J, Benton A, Lathia N, Howarth E, Jones PB. · The Lancet. Public health · 2018 · Single-blind randomized controlled trial (outcome assessors blinded)

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In the 616-student Mindful Student Study, an eight-week course reduced exam-period psychological distress relative to usual support. The adjusted difference was -0.14 CORE-OM points, with a 95% confidence interval of -0.22 to -0.06.

Recorded limitations

Only 74% completed the exam-period outcome, and 59% of assigned students attended at least half the course. Participants knew allocation and the comparator did not match instructor attention.
