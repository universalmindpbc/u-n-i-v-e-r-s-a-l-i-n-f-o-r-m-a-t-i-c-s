Surface code scaling on heavy-hex superconducting quantum processors

Arian Vezvaee; Cesar Benito; Mario Morford-Oberst; Alejandro Bermudez; Daniel A. Lidar · Nature Communications · 2026 · Superconducting quantum-processor experiment and modelling

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The authors combine surface-code layouts and pulse control on IBM heavy-hex processors. Increasing code size along one direction improves protection of particular logical states. Their analysis shows why both state directions and coherent noise matter when assessing error suppression.

Recorded limitations

Directional improvements do not establish general below-threshold scaling. The paper identifies further noise reduction needed for balanced scaling. It does not test Fibonacci or M9 P24 sequences.
