Local stabilizer codes in three dimensions without string logical operators

Jeongwan Haah · Physical Review A · 2011 · Theoretical three-dimensional stabilizer codes

Original publication

Recorded summary

Haah constructs local stabilizer codes in three dimensions without string-like logical operators. The work introduces a different geometric organisation of information and errors, associated with the fractal structure of logical operations in the cubic-code family.

Recorded limitations

This is code theory, not evidence that any fractal pattern stabilises a physical quantum computer. It does not demonstrate a Fibonacci connection or a complete self-correcting memory operating at finite temperature.
