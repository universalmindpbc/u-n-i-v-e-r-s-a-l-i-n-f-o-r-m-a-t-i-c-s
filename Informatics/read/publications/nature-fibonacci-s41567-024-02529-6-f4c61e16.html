Non-Abelian braiding of Fibonacci anyons with a superconducting processor

Shibo Xu; Zheng-Zhi Sun; Ke Wang; Hekang Li; Zitian Zhu; Hang Dong; Jinfeng Deng; Xu Zhang; Jiachen Chen; Yaozu Wu; Chuanyu Zhang; Feitong Jin; Xuhao Zhu; Yu Gao; Aosai Zhang; Ning Wang; Yiren Zou; Ziqi Tan; Fanhao Shen; Jiarun Zhong; Zehang Bao; Weikang Li; Wenjie Jiang; Li-Wei Yu; Zixuan Song; Pengfei Zhang; Liang Xiang; Qiujiang Guo; Zhen Wang; Chao Song; H. Wang; Dong-Ling Deng · Nature Physics · 2024 · Superconducting-processor quantum simulation experiment

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A 27-qubit superconducting processor prepares Fibonacci string-net states and probes their topological properties. Created excitations exhibit fusion and braiding signatures consistent with Fibonacci anyons.

Recorded limitations

The demonstration uses digitally prepared states on a noisy processor. It does not establish a scalable fault-tolerant computer or a biological role for Fibonacci mathematics.
