Mindfulness Meditation Reduces Intraocular Pressure, Lowers Stress Biomarkers and Modulates Gene Expression in Glaucoma: A Randomized Controlled Trial.

Dada T, Mittal D, Mohanty K, Faiq MA, Bhat MA, Yadav RK, Sihota R, Sidhu T, Velpandian T, Kalaivani M, Pandey RM, Gao Y, Sabel BA, Dada R. · Journal of glaucoma · 2018 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 90 patients with primary open-angle glaucoma, a short mindfulness programme reduced intraocular pressure relative to waitlist and reported associated stress-biomarker and gene-expression changes.

Recorded limitations

A small, short-duration adjunctive trial with a waitlist comparator. Expression and biomarker associations do not prove mediation of pressure reduction or broad effects across diseases.
