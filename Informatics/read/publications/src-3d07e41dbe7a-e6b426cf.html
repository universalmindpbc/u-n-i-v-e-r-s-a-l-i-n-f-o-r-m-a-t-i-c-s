The efficacy of trauma-based therapies for Aboriginal children and young people in out-of-home care: a journey through LINKS

NSW Department of Communities and Justice · 2022 · Programme evaluation presentation

Original publication

Recorded summary

The presentation reports 159 assessment completers from October 2017 to August 2020: 77 Aboriginal and 82 non-Aboriginal children aged 3–16. Referrals were voluntary through caseworkers for children experiencing placement instability, increased respite needs or repeated placement changes.

Recorded limitations

Treatment allocation was not randomized. Different starting scores and participant characteristics limit comparisons across therapies and clinician groups.
