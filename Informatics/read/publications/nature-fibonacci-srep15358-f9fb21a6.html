Biophysical optimality of the golden angle in phyllotaxis

Takuya Okabe · Scientific Reports · 2015 · Mathematical and numerical model of plant development

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

An energy-cost model identifies the golden angle as an optimum for transitions between phyllotactic fractions and reproduces several common and less common leaf-arrangement sequences.

Recorded limitations

The proposed evolutionary explanation depends on the chosen transition-cost model. Agreement with known patterns does not measure the assumed energy costs or establish the genetic mechanism.
