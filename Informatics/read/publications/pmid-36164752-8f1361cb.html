Glial cell response to constant low light exposure in rat retina

Bruera MG, Benedetto MM, Guido ME, Degano AL, Contin MA. · Visual neuroscience · 2022 · Rat constant-light exposure experiment

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Prolonged constant low light was followed by retinal degeneration, glial activation and increased immune-mediator expression, including IL-6 and TNF.

Recorded limitations

This is a retinal-damage model. It does not establish that ordinary visual activity produces the same changes, or that increased expression is beneficial.
