Mindfulness-based cognitive therapy versus treatment as usual after non-remission with NHS Talking Therapies high-intensity psychological therapy for depression: a UK-based clinical effectiveness and cost-effectiveness randomised, controlled, superiority trial

Barnhofer T, Dunn BD, Strauss C, Ruths FA, Barrett B, Ryan M, Ladwa A, Stafford F, Fichera R, Baber H, McGuinness A, Metcalfe I, Kan DKY, Pooley J, Harding D, Tassie E, Carson J, Rhodes S, Young AH, Connors J, Warren FC. · The lancet. Psychiatry · 2025 · Randomized controlled trial; masked statistical analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In RESPOND, MBCT plus usual care reduced depression symptoms more than usual care at 34 weeks: an adjusted PHQ-9 difference of −2.49 points (95% CI −3.89 to −1.09).

Recorded limitations

The control received usual care rather than a matched group intervention. The study assesses symptoms and costs; it does not test gene expression.
