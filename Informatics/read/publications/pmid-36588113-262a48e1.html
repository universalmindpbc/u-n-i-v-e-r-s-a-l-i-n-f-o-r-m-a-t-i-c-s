LIGHTSITE II Randomized Multicenter Trial: Evaluation of Multiwavelength Photobiomodulation in Non-exudative Age-Related Macular Degeneration

Burton B, Parodi MB, Jürgens I, Zanlonghi X, Hornan D, Roider J, Lorenz K, Munk MR, Croissant CL, Tedford SE, Walker M, Ruckert R, Tedford CE. · Ophthalmology and therapy · 2023 · Randomized sham-controlled multicentre trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The trial assessed repeated multiwavelength photobiomodulation in non-exudative macular degeneration and reported visual-acuity and anatomical outcomes.

Recorded limitations

This clinical trial did not measure target-gene expression. The reported complete-treatment analyses and eye-level outcomes need to be interpreted within the trial design.
