Remote EMDR versus CBT for PTSD after the Kahramanmaraş earthquakes: a randomized trial.

Çınaroğlu M, Yılmazer E, Ülker SV, Sayar GH. · Frontiers in Psychiatry · 2026 · Randomized controlled trial · open-label

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 89 randomized earthquake survivors, remote EMDR and CBT reduced PTSD, depression and anxiety scores relative to waitlist across 12 sessions.

Recorded limitations

Outcomes were self-reported. Therapists and participants knew allocation; assessor/analyst masking does not blind the people reporting symptoms. Direct superiority comparisons were not the trial’s primary power target.
