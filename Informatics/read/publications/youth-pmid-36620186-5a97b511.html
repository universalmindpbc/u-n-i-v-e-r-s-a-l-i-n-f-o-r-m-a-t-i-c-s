Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Themes: privacy, user empowerment, and engagement rather than achievement; participants wanted choice about seeking professional help.

Recorded limitations

Small purposive sample, stated preferences rather than measured app retention or clinical effects; higher-risk young people excluded.
