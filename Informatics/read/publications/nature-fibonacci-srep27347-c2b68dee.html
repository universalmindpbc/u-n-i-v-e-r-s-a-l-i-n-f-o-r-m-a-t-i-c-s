Optimal hash arrangement of tentacles in jellyfish

Takuya Okabe; Jin Yoshimura · Scientific Reports · 2016 · Biological observations with a mathematical optimality model

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The authors relate previously reported jellyfish tentacle arrangements to Fibonacci hashing and derive an angular-spacing model that distributes a changing number of organs uniformly.

Recorded limitations

The biological observations come from earlier studies. Constant-angle development and a fitness advantage for uniform spacing are model assumptions; a controlled evolutionary test is absent.
