Digital Mental Health Interventions for the Prevention and Treatment of Social Anxiety Disorder in Children, Adolescents, and Young Adults: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials.

Walder N, Frey A, Berger T, Schmidt SJ. · Journal of medical Internet research · 2025 · Systematic review / meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Digital social-anxiety interventions showed a moderate pooled effect, especially versus waitlist; guided and disorder-specific subgroups had larger estimates.

Recorded limitations

Only three studies rated low risk of bias, heterogeneity and variable adherence. Cross-study guidance comparisons are not randomized proof that guidance causes superior outcomes.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
