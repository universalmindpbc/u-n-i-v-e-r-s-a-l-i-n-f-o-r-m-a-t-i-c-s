Appropriate Use and Operationalization of Adherence to Digital Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Depression and Anxiety in Youth: Systematic Review.

Li SH, Achilles MR, Werner-Seidler A, Beames JR, Subotic-Kerry M, O'Dea B. · JMIR mental health · 2022 · Review / synthesis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Instructions for appropriate use were poorly standardized, and greater usage was not consistently associated with better outcomes.

Recorded limitations

Heterogeneous adherence definitions and limited studies testing outcome associations; CBT-specific evidence does not validate any digital product.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
