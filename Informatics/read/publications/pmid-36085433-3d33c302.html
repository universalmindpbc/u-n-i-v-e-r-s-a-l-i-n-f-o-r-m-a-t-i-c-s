Developmental light deprivation transiently reduces the expression of vGluT2 and GluN2B in the rat ventral suprachiasmatic nucleus

Reyes-Méndez ME, Herrera-Zamora JM, Osuna-Lopez F, Aguilar-Martínez IS, Góngora-Alfaro JL, Navarro-Polanco RA, Sánchez-Pastor E, Moreno-Galindo EG, Alamilla J. · Synapse (New York, N.Y.) · 2023 · Developmental light-deprivation experiment in rats

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Constant-darkness rearing transiently reduced vGluT2 and GluN2B expression in the ventral suprachiasmatic nucleus; the differences were compensated at later stages.

Recorded limitations

Developmental protein expression in rats is not direct evidence of human GRIN2B transcription or an adult visual therapy effect.
