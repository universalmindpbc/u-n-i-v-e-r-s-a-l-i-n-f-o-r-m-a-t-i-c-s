Psychological assessments, allostatic load and gene expression analyses in a randomized controlled trial comparing meditation, yoga, and stress education

Denninger JW, Joss D, Romero PM, Khalsa SBS, Hoge EA, Bhasin M, Lazar SW, Dusek JA, Macklin E, Libermann T, Fricchione GL, Benson H. · Frontiers in Psychology · 2025 · Randomized controlled trial; registered double-blind (participants and outcome assessors)

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The three-arm trial found no significant between-group effects on psychological, genomic or allostatic-load outcomes. Most psychological measures improved within each group. Circulating IL-6 increased slightly within the meditation group at follow-up, remaining in the normal range.

Recorded limitations

Within-group improvement does not establish a treatment advantage. Participants were stressed but otherwise healthy; results may differ in clinical populations.
