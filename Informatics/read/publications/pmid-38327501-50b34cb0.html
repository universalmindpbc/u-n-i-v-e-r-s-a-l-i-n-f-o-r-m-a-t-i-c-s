Randomized controlled trials of mind-body interventions for posttraumatic stress disorder: a systematic review

Kaplan J, Somohano VC, Zaccari B, O'Neil ME. · Frontiers in Psychology · 2023 · Systematic review of RCTs

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Meditation- and mantra-based interventions had moderate strength of evidence versus passive controls in the PTSD trial record reviewed by VA-affiliated researchers.

Recorded limitations

Comparator quality, heterogeneous measures and limited objective outcomes constrain interpretation.
