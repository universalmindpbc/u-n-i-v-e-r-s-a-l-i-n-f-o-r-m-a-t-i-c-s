A promising approach using Fibonacci sequence-based optimization algorithms and advanced computing

H. Tran-Ngoc; T. Le-Xuan; S. Khatir; G. De Roeck; T. Bui-Tien; Magd Abdel Wahab · Scientific Reports · 2023 · Numerical optimization and structural-model case study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A Fibonacci-based modification of the Salp Swarm Algorithm is evaluated for structural-health monitoring of a railway bridge, alongside parallel computation and vectorization intended to reduce computational cost.

Recorded limitations

The reported performance is specific to the tested algorithms and bridge-model setting. The study does not isolate a general superiority of Fibonacci methods or validate broad biological claims mentioned in its background.
