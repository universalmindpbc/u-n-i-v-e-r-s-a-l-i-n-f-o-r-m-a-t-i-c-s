Activation of oxytocin receptors in mouse GABAergic amacrine cells modulates retinal dopaminergic signaling

Hu S, Wang Y, Han X, Dai M, Zhang Y, Ma Y, Weng S, Xiao L, Xiao L. · BMC biology · 2022 · Mouse retinal molecular and physiological experiments

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Oxtr mRNA and receptor protein were detected in retinal amacrine cells. Applying oxytocin activated dopaminergic cells and altered retinal responses through receptor-dependent signalling.

Recorded limitations

This establishes a retinal signalling context. It does not show that a visual pattern changes OXTR transcription in humans.
