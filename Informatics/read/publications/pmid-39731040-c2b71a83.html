The effect of eye movement desensitization on neurocognitive functioning compared to retrieval-only in PTSD patients: a randomized controlled trial.

Susanty E, Sijbrandij M, Srisayekti W, Suparman Y, Huizink AC. · BMC psychiatry · 2024 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Ninety-one patients received eye movement desensitisation or retrieval alone; no additional neurocognitive benefit from eye movements was found.

Recorded limitations

The tested neurocognitive endpoints and component intervention do not represent every outcome or the full clinical protocol.
