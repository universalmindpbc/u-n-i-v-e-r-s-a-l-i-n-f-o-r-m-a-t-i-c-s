The Penrose Tiling is a Quantum Error-Correcting Code

Zhi Li; Latham Boyle · arXiv · 2024 · Theoretical preprint; version 2

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Li and Boyle construct quantum error-correcting codes from Penrose tilings and variants based on Ammann–Beenker and Fibonacci tilings. Their construction connects nonperiodic geometric structure with the ability to recover information after local errors or erasures.

Recorded limitations

This is a mathematical construction in a preprint, not an experimental demonstration on Aquila. Its Fibonacci tilings are different from the Atlas M9 P24 sequences. A physical implementation and its resource requirements remain separate questions.
