Assessment and Management of Conditions Specifically related to Stress

World Health Organisation and UNHCR · Clinical guidance

Original publication · Original publication

Attributed extracts from the research record

“EMDR is an advanced psychological intervention for stress and PTSD.
Like CBT-T, EMDR aims to reduce subjective distress and strengthen adaptive beliefs related to the traumatic event. Unlike CBT-T, EMDR does not involve (a) detailed descriptions of the event, (b) direct challenging of beliefs, (c) extended exposure or (d) homework.”
