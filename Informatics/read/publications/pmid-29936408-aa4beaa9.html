Effect of meditation on psychological distress and brain functioning: A randomized controlled study.

Travis F, Valosek L, Konrad A, Link J, Salerno J, Scheller R, Nidich S. · Brain and cognition · 2018 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 96 school-district staff, Transcendental Meditation improved mood and distress relative to waitlist over four months. An EEG subset of 79 participants also showed changes in the study's Brain Integration Scale.

Recorded limitations

The EEG composite is a study-specific measure, not proof of globally improved brain function. A waitlist does not isolate practice-specific effects from support and expectancy.
