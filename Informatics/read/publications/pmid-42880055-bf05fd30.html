Efficacy of eye movement desensitization and reprocessing for anxiety: An umbrella review and meta-meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.

Antuña-Camblor C, Rabito-Alcón MF, Mathias-Fonseca TF. · Journal of anxiety disorders · 2026 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Eleven meta-analyses report an average anxiety reduction, with a smaller advantage against active psychological treatments.

Recorded limitations

Most comparisons involve PTSD, nine reviews were rated low or critically low quality, and heterogeneity was substantial.
