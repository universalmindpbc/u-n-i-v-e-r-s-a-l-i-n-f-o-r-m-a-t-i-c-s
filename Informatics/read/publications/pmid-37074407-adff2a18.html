Photobiomodulation at 660 nm promotes collagen synthesis via downregulation of HIF-1α expression without photodamage in human scleral fibroblasts in vitro in a hypoxic environment

Zhang P, Zhang X, Zhu H. · Graefe's archive for clinical and experimental ophthalmology = Albrecht von Graefes Archiv fur klinische und experimentelle Ophthalmologie · 2023 · Human scleral fibroblast experiment in vitro

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In hypoxic human scleral fibroblasts, 660 nm photobiomodulation increased collagen and reduced HIF-1α protein. HIF-1α overexpression attenuated the response.

Recorded limitations

The reported assays concern protein and cellular responses, not clinical myopia outcomes or direct COL1A1 RNA quantification.
