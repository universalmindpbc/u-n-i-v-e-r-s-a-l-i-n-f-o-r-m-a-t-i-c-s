Treatment of traumatic birth experience with postpartum early eye movement desensitization and reprocessing therapy: a randomized clinical trial.

Hendrix YMGA, van Pampus MG, Hofman A, Henrichs J, van der Horst HE, de Jongh A. · American journal of obstetrics and gynecology · 2025 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Of 151 women randomised after a traumatic birth, 143 entered the reported intention-to-treat analysis. Two early postpartum EMDR sessions reduced self-reported and clinician-rated PTSD symptom severity compared with two telephone contacts; the reported raw symptom differences were 8.7 and 3.8 points respectively.

Recorded limitations

Clinician-diagnosed PTSD rates did not differ significantly. The abstract contains an inconsistent confidence-interval entry, so that interval is not reproduced here; follow-up was nine weeks postpartum.
