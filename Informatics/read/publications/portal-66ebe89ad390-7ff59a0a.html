Is the Group Traumatic Episode Protocol (G-TEP) Effective for Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder? A Meta-Analysis.

Dalkılınç G, Satıcı B. · Clinical psychology & psychotherapy · 2026 · Systematic review + meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Twelve G-TEP studies produced a large pooled reduction in PTSD symptoms (Hedges' g=1.28), supporting a feasible group option particularly after collective trauma.

Recorded limitations

The synthesis covers G-TEP generally, not online delivery alone or a veterans-only population, and the included methods and samples differed.
