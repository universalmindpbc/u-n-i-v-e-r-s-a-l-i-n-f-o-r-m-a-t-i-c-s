Effects of eye movement desensitisation and reprocessing on depressive symptoms, stress and rumination in adolescents with depression: a randomised controlled trial.

Zheng S, Shen Y, Geng F, Ye M, Song S, Wang R, Zhang S, Ou Y, Zhou X. · European journal of psychotraumatology · 2025 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Thirty adolescents aged 12–18 completed a comparison of EMDR plus an SSRI with an SSRI alone. Six sessions over two weeks were associated with larger improvements in depression, event-related stress and rumination.

Recorded limitations

Small trial with brief follow-up. This is adjunctive treatment alongside medication; it does not test an entertainment intervention.
