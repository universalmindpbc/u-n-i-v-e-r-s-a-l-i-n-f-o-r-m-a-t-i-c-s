Effects of eye movement desensitisation and reprocessing on depressive symptoms, stress and rumination in adolescents with depression: a randomised controlled trial.

Zheng S, Shen Y, Geng F, Ye M, Song S, Wang R, Zhang S, Ou Y, Zhou X. · European journal of psychotraumatology · 2025 · Randomized controlled trial report · double blinding not verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The add-on EMDR arm improved more on depression, event-related distress and rumination over time. This tests EMDR with medication and hospital care, not stand-alone treatment or a community wellbeing activity.

Recorded limitations

Small sample, short follow-up, no attention-matched psychotherapy control and unclear assessor masking.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
