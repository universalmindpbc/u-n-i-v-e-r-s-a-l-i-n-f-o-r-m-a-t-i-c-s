Fractional Transport of Photons in Deterministic Aperiodic Structures

Luca Dal Negro; Sandeep Inampudi · Scientific Reports · 2017 · Numerical wave transport and analytical fractional-dynamics modelling

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Transfer-matrix simulations compare photon propagation in deterministic aperiodic structures. Fibonacci and Thue–Morse stacks exhibit anomalous power-law transport, while Rudin–Shapiro structures exhibit a distinct logarithmic regime.

Recorded limitations

These are modelled transport regimes under specified optical assumptions. The logarithmic result is associated with Rudin–Shapiro structures, not a finding unique to Fibonacci sequences.
