The Effects of Meditation, Yoga, and Mindfulness on Depression, Anxiety, and Stress in Tertiary Education Students: A Meta-Analysis.

Breedvelt JJF, Amanvermez Y, Harrer M, Karyotaki E, Gilbody S, Bockting CLH, Cuijpers P, Ebert DD. · Frontiers in psychiatry · 2019 · Systematic review / meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Moderate effects on depression/anxiety/stress versus broad controls; active-control effect g=0.13 (95% CI −0.18 to 0.43) not conclusive.

Recorded limitations

Most studies high risk of bias; intervention heterogeneity; no RCT reported safety; academic effects uncertain.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
