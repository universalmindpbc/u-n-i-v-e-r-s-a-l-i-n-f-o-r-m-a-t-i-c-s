The photoreceptor mosaic

P K Ahnelt · Eye · 1998 · Anatomical review of the human retinal photoreceptor mosaic

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Ahnelt describes how human rods and cones are distributed across the retina. Cone packing is especially dense near the fovea; different cone populations have different arrangements. This provides biological context for exploring local packing and sampling geometry in Vision Atlas.

Recorded limitations

The review does not demonstrate a universal perfect hexagonal retina, tetrahedral visual processing, or a Fibonacci mechanism. It is anatomical evidence, not an EMDR treatment trial or a quantum-stability experiment.
