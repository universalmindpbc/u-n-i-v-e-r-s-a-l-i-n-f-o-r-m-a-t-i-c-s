Randomized Controlled Trial: Self-Care Traumatic Episode Protocol, Computerized EMDR Treatment of COVID-19-Related Stress

Judy Moench; Olivia Billsten · Journal of EMDR Practice and Research · 2021 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication

Attributed extracts from the research record

“The randomized controlled trial (RCT) indicated that STEP may be effective in increasing general self-efficacy and reducing symptoms of depression, anxiety, and stress among Mental Health clinicians in the context of COVID-19.”

Recorded limitations

STEP tested 34 mental-health clinicians using a recorded session with worksheets, clinical screening and access to support. This population and protocol differ from general unguided entertainment use.
