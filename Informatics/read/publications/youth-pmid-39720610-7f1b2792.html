Adherence and efficacy outcomes in young Australians with suicidal ideation using a self-management app and digital engagement strategy compared with a sham app: a three-arm randomised controlled trial.

Torok M, McGillivray L, Gan DZQ, Han J, Hetrick S, Wong QJJ. · EClinicalMedicine · 2025 · Randomized controlled trial report · verified double blind

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Combined active arms reduced suicidal-ideation severity at 60 days (d=.48) and 120 days (d=.29), not 30 days. Extra engagement resources did not improve module completion or efficacy.

Recorded limitations

Specific clinical app and selected English-speaking smartphone users; ideation reduction does not establish prevention of suicide or whole-lab benefit; engagement comparison may be distinguishable.
