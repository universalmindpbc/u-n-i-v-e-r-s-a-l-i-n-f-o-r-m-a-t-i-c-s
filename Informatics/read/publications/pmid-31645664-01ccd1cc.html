Successful treatment of post-traumatic stress disorder reverses DNA methylation marks.

Vinkers CH, Geuze E, van Rooij SJH, Kennis M, Schür RR, Nispeling DM, Smith AK, Nievergelt CM, Uddin M, Rutten BPF, Vermetten E, Boks MP. · Molecular Psychiatry · 2019 · Longitudinal molecular cohort

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In veterans receiving trauma-focused psychotherapy, remission was associated with changes in 12 blood DNA-methylation regions. ZFP57 also showed evidence in an independent prospective deployment cohort.

Recorded limitations

Treatment included trauma-focused CBT and/or EMDR. This observational analysis does not isolate EMDR or establish altered gene expression from methylation alone.
