Direct Observation of the Zigzag Edge States of a Supramolecular Diatomic Kagome Lattice

Ryohei Nemoto; Xiangzhi Meng; Behzad Mortezapour; Alexander Weismann; Saya Nakano; Masahisa Tsuchiizu; Ryuichi Arafune; Noriaki Takagi; Sho Nakamura; Katsunori Wakabayashi; Rie Suizu; Richard Berndt; Takashi Uchihashi; Kunio Awaga · Nano Letters · 2026 · Molecular-lattice microscopy and tight-binding calculations

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The authors assembled a molecular diatomic kagome lattice and observed electronic states along its zigzag edges. Microscopy and calculations connect these states to the lattice's electronic structure and topology.

Recorded limitations

This is a quantum-materials result, not a demonstration of a longer-lived qubit or an error-correcting computer. It provides an example of geometry changing electronic behaviour, without establishing a Fibonacci mechanism.
