Do mindfulness-based programmes improve the cognitive skills, behaviour and mental health of children and adolescents? An updated meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials.

Dunning D, Tudor K, Radley L, Dalrymple N, Funk J, Vainre M, Ford T, Montero-Marin J, Kuyken W, Dalgleish T. · Evidence-based mental health · 2022 · Systematic review / meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Small effects versus passive controls; active-control anxiety/stress d=0.11. No significant positive effects in follow-up studies.

Recorded limitations

Generally low-quality/inconclusive evidence, insufficient harms reporting and no new published protocol for update; overlaps earlier Dunning review.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
