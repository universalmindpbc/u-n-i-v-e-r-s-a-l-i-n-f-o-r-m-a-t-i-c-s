Peer-delivered combined EMD and imagery rescripting for traumatized Chinese university students: a RCT.

Fan H, Zhang J, Wang Y, Yang K, Wang Y, Yang X. · European journal of psychotraumatology · 2026 · Randomized controlled trial · open-label

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Ninety-five university students were randomized to peer-delivered EMD plus imagery rescripting, imagery rescripting alone or care as usual. Four supervised sessions produced similar changes in depression and anxiety across groups; the combined intervention better maintained resilience gains at follow-up.

Recorded limitations

Trained peers received clinical supervision. The trial tested a combined intervention, with no significant group-by-time effects for depression, anxiety or self-efficacy.
