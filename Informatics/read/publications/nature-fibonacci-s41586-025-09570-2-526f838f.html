Heat-rechargeable computation in DNA logic circuits and neural networks

Tianqi Song; Lulu Qian · Nature · 2025 · Engineered DNA-circuit experiments with simulations

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Heat resets engineered DNA circuits for repeated computation. A feedforward logic circuit returns the first 16 Fibonacci-word elements from index inputs and maintains performance across 16 computation rounds with 15 resets.

Recorded limitations

The Fibonacci circuit uses a designed truth table and finite inputs. It does not autonomously generate an unbounded sequence or reveal a naturally occurring genomic computation.
