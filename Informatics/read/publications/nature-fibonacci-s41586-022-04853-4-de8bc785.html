Dynamical topological phase realized in a trapped-ion quantum simulator

Philipp T. Dumitrescu; Justin G. Bohnet; John P. Gaebler; Aaron Hankin; David Hayes; Ajesh Kumar; Brian Neyenhuis; Romain Vasseur; Andrew C. Potter · Nature · 2022 · Trapped-ion quantum-simulation experiment

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A quasiperiodic Fibonacci drive creates a dynamical topological phase in a ten-qubit trapped-ion chain. Edge-qubit behaviour exhibits protection against specified control imperfections relative to a periodic-drive comparison.

Recorded limitations

The result concerns a finite quantum simulator and particular dynamics. It does not demonstrate general-purpose fault tolerance; the present appraisal covers the abstract and accessible extended-data descriptions.
