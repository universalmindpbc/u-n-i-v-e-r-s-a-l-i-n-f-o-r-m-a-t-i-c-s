Health Care Workers' Need for Headspace: Findings From a Multisite Definitive Randomized Controlled Trial of an Unguided Digital Mindfulness-Based Self-help App to Reduce Healthcare Worker Stress.

Taylor H, Cavanagh K, Field AP, Strauss C. · JMIR mHealth and uHealth · 2022 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 2,182 NHS staff, a digital mindfulness app produced a small but statistically significant reduction in stress relative to an active digital control over 4.5 months, with additional benefits on several wellbeing measures.

Recorded limitations

Effects were small and burnout and sickness absence did not improve significantly. Engagement was limited; this supports a scalable adjunct rather than a substitute for organisational support.
