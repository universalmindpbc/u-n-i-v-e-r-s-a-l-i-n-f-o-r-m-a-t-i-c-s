Epigenetics of traumatic stress: The association of NR3C1 methylation and posttraumatic stress disorder symptom changes in response to narrative exposure therapy

Wilker S, Vukojevic V, Schneider A, Pfeiffer A, Inerle S, Pauly M, Elbert T, Papassotiropoulos A, de Quervain D, Kolassa IT. · Translational Psychiatry · 2023 · Longitudinal psychotherapy cohort

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Change at NR3C1 CpG cg25535999 was associated with PTSD symptom change after correction for multiple comparisons. Responders showed increased methylation at this site.

Recorded limitations

The study examines a complete therapy programme and an association with recovery. It does not establish a causal effect of individual words or show NR3C1 RNA changes.
