Attributed extracts from the research record

“DJ Snake, went live from Bangladesh yesterday in a plight to raise netizen’s attention to the over one million Rohingya refugees from Myanmar”
“The French DJ and producer is currently there walking among the Rohingya refugees in their camps and posted a video encouraging his fans to help raise resources for the stateless minority that would help improve living conditions by providing food, education, medicine, clothing, shelter and more.”
“Together with activist network Love Army, founded by Jérôme Jarre who believes the everyone with an online audience has a voice, yesterday they went live and met with refugees, talked to them about their experiences and learned about their needs.”
