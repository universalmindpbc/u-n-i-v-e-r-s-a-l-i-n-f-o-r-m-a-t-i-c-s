Effects of Listening to Binaural Beats on Anxiety Levels and Pain Scores in Male Patients Undergoing Cystoscopy and Ureteral Stent Removal: A Randomized Placebo-Controlled Trial.

Ölçücü MT, Yılmaz K, Karamık K, Okuducu Y, Özsoy Ç, Aktaş Y, Çakır S, Ateş M. · Journal of endourology · 2021 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In men undergoing cystoscopy or ureteral stent removal, binaural beats reduced pain compared with classical music and silent headphones; both sound interventions improved anxiety relative to silence.

Recorded limitations

The analysed procedure groups totalled 352 participants after exclusions. Procedure tolerance was less favourable with binaural beats, and results are specific to these urological settings.
