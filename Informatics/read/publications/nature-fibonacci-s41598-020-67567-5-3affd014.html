Explosive Fibonacci-sequence growth into unusual sector-face morphology in poly(l-lactic acid) crystallized with polymeric diluents

Graecia Lugito; Selvaraj Nagarajan; Eamor M. Woo · Scientific Reports · 2020 · Polymer-crystallization microscopy and scattering experiments

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In diluted poly(l-lactic acid) crystals, microscopy reveals branching lamellar sectors whose branch counts grow approximately along a Fibonacci sequence. Thermal and scattering measurements characterize the resulting structures.

Recorded limitations

The authors describe approximate counts and acknowledge difficulty distinguishing branches. The polymers and crystallization conditions are specific; this is not evidence for a universal exact growth law.
