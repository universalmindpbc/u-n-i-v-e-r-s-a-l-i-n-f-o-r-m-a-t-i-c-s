Effect of Mindfulness Versus Loving-kindness Training on Leukocyte Gene Expression in Midlife Adults Raised in Low-Socioeconomic Status Households

West TN, Zhou J, Brantley MM, Kim SL, Brantley J, Salzberg S, Cole SW, Fredrickson BL. · Mindfulness · 2022 · Randomized controlled trial; registered triple-masked

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

CTRA expression declined in the mindfulness group and increased in the loving-kindness group, opposite to the investigators’ prediction. Positive emotions increased in both groups.

Recorded limitations

The comparison distinguishes two meditation programmes and lacks a no-treatment arm. Changes in positive emotion did not explain the divergent molecular result.
