Efficacy of the Adapted Integrative Group EMDR Therapy Protocol for Ongoing Traumatic Stress in Adolescents after a Traumatic Event.

Almarcha GS, Peinado AI, de Álvaro JI, López-Morales JL, Reyes-Bossio M, Delgado-Campusano M. · Journal of child & adolescent trauma · 2026 · Uncontrolled before-and-after study

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Eleven school students meeting PTSD criteria received psychoeducation and psychologist-led group EMDR. PTSD symptoms fell following the programme.

Recorded limitations

Only 11 of 235 screened students entered treatment. No control group isolates treatment effects.
