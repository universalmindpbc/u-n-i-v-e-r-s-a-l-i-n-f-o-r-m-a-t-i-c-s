Longitudinal DNA methylation in parent-infant pairs impacted by intergenerational social adversity: An RCT of the Michigan Model of Infant Mental Health Home Visiting

Petroff RL, Jester J, Riggs J, Alfafara E, Springer K, Kerr N, Issa M, Hall A, Rosenblum K, Goodrich JM, Muzik M, Michigan Collaborative for Infant Mental Health Research. · Brain and behavior · 2024 · Randomized parent–infant psychotherapy trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Infant BDNF methylation showed a possible decrease after the home-visiting psychotherapy programme. Most maternal molecular associations and intervention effects were absent.

Recorded limitations

The intervention includes multiple relational components. The results do not isolate particular spoken words, and the molecular findings were mixed.
