Effects of Mobile Mindfulness Meditation on the Mental Health of University Students: Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.

Chen B, Yang T, Xiao L, Xu C, Zhu C. · Journal of medical Internet research · 2023 · Systematic review / meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Stress SMD −0.41 and anxiety −0.29; depression and resilience effects not established.

Recorded limitations

Moderate-to-low certainty; includes non-randomised study; very large mindfulness estimate and questionable masking classifications (Calm primary study explicitly unblinded) reduce confidence. Do not reuse extreme mindfulness effect estimate.

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
