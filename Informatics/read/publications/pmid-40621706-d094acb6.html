Effects of Auditory Frequency Stimulation on Balance and Rehabilitation Outcomes in Patients With Stroke: A Randomized Case-Control Study.

Chen R, Jin L, Song J, Dong J, Li Y. · Brain and behavior · 2025 · Randomized controlled pilot study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Among 27 stroke rehabilitation completers, the binaural-stimulation group improved daily-living and depression scores more than the music comparison group. Balance measures improved in both groups.

Recorded limitations

The small sample and combined rehabilitation context limit causal attribution to the beats. Correlations between functional measures do not prove a brain mechanism.
