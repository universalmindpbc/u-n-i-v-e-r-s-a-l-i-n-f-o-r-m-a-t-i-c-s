Efficacy of the Mindfulness Meditation Mobile App "Calm" to Reduce Stress Among College Students: Randomized Controlled Trial.

Huberty J, Green J, Glissmann C, Larkey L, Puzia M, Lee C. · JMIR mHealth and uHealth · 2019 · Randomized controlled trial report · double blinding not verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Stress, mindfulness and self-compassion improved versus waitlist and largely persisted to 12 weeks; most other health behaviours not improved.

Recorded limitations

Small self-selected stressed student sample, inactive control, self-report and commercial-app setting; actual practice below prescribed dose.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
