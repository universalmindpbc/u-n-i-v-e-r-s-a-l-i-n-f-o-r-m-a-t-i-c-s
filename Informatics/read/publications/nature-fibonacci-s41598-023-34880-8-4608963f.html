Golden ratio in venation patterns of dragonfly wings

Keene Lu; Samson Shen; Lisa M. Miller; Xiaojing Huang · Scientific Reports · 2023 · Observational morphology with a geometric model

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Measurements from 50 dragonfly wings reveal preferred intervein angles. A golden-angle and golden-ratio partition model describes selected features of the angle distributions.

Recorded limitations

Geometric agreement is observational. The analysis does not experimentally establish a developmental cause or prove that this arrangement optimizes wing performance.
