The Role of the Endocannabinoids 2-AG and Anandamide in Clinical Symptoms and Treatment Outcome in Veterans with PTSD.

Leen NA, de Weijer AD, van Rooij SJH, Kennis M, Baas JMP, Geuze E. · Chronic stress (Thousand Oaks, Calif.) · 2022 · Longitudinal biomarker cohort

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Plasma anandamide and 2-AG were measured in 54 veterans with PTSD and 26 combat controls. Pretreatment levels did not predict symptom reduction following trauma-focused therapy; cortisol decreased over time.

Recorded limitations

Treatments included CBT with exposure or EMDR. Exploratory correlations require replication; endocannabinoid concentrations are distinct from gene expression.
