MicroRNAs expression changes associated with trauma-focused psychotherapy efficacy in treatment-resistant depression: An exploratory study.

Buson L, Carvalho Silva R, Galbiati C, Bortolomasi M, Scaglia M, Perusi G, Abate M, Maffioletti E, Gennarelli M, Lorenzon N, Dierssen M, European College of Neuropsychopharmacology (ECNP) Pharmacogenomics & Transcriptomics Network, Minelli A. · Neuroscience applied · 2026 · Exploratory longitudinal microRNA study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 24 people receiving EMDR or trauma-focused CBT for treatment-resistant depression, 148 microRNAs passed quality control. The combined sample’s miR-132-3p change survived correction for multiple testing.

Recorded limitations

EMDR-only subgroup changes did not survive multiple-testing correction. The result does not establish an EMDR-specific microRNA effect.
