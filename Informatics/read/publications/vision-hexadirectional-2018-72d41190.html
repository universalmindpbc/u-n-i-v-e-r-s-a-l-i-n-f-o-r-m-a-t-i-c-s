Hexadirectional coding of visual space in human entorhinal cortex

Matthias Nau; Tobias Navarro Schröder; Jacob L. S. Bellmund; Christian F. Doeller · Nature Neuroscience · 2018 · Human fMRI during controlled visual tracking

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Nau and colleagues found a sixfold directional pattern in human entorhinal-cortex activity during a visual tracking task. The result supports a grid-like representation of visual space and gives Vision Atlas a measured example of sixfold organisation in brain activity.

Recorded limitations

An fMRI population signal is not a direct image of individual grid cells or proof of a tetrahedral eye mechanism. This study does not test Fibonacci sequences, EMDR outcomes, or quantum error correction.
