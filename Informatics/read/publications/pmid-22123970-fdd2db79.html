Common oxytocin receptor gene (OXTR) polymorphism and social support interact to reduce stress in humans

Chen FS, Kumsta R, von Dawans B, Monakhov M, Ebstein RP, Heinrichs M. · Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America · 2011

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Attributed extracts from the research record

“Differential stress responses between the genotype groups were observed depending on the presence or absence of social support.”
“Only individuals with one or two copies of the G allele of rs53576 showed lower cortisol responses to stress after social support, compared with individuals with the same genotype receiving no social support.”
“These results indicate that genetic variation of the oxytocin system modulates the effectiveness of positive social interaction as a protective buffer against a stressful experience.”
