Oxytocin expression and function in the posterior retina: a novel signaling pathway

Halbach P, Pillers DA, York N, Asuma MP, Chiu MA, Luo W, Tokarz S, Bird IM, Pattnaik BR. · Investigative ophthalmology & visual science · 2015 · Human and rhesus tissue; cultured human retinal cells

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The study identified OXT/OXTR signalling components in posterior retinal tissues and a calcium response to oxytocin in cultured retinal pigment epithelial cells.

Recorded limitations

This is a foundational 2015 study, added for context. Receptor presence and activation do not demonstrate visual-exposure-induced gene expression.
