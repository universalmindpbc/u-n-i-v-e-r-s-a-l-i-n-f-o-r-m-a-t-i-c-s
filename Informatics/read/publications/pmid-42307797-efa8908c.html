Effects of binaural beats on acute stress recovery and autonomic regulation in multiple sclerosis: a pilot double-blind randomized controlled trial.

Vaheb S, Hatef B, Mirani A, Shaygannejad V, Pirzad Jahromi G. · Neurological sciences : official journal of the Italian Neurological Society and of the Italian Society of Clinical Neurophysiology · 2026 · Double-blind randomized controlled pilot trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A pilot involving 30 people with multiple sclerosis and 30 healthy participants reported lower salivary cortisol after 4 Hz binaural stimulation than sham following an experimental stress challenge, alongside autonomic and EEG changes.

Recorded limitations

Small, mixed participant groups and acute follow-up limit generalisation. Cortisol is a hormonal biomarker; this study did not measure gene expression or demonstrate long-term clinical benefit.
