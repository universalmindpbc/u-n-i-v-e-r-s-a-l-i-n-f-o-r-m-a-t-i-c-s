Effects of Music and White Noise Exposure on the Gut Microbiota, Oxidative Stress, and Immune-Related Gene Expression of Mice

Zhang Z, Wu Y, Zhou S, Fu P, Yan H. · Microorganisms · 2023 · Controlled mouse experiment

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Music and white-noise exposures produced different immune-related, microbial and oxidative-stress profiles in mice.

Recorded limitations

Animal exposure conditions do not establish a human music benefit. Gene assays, microbial composition and serum enzyme activity are distinct outcomes.
