Music compensates for altered gene expression in age-related cognitive disorders

Gómez-Carballa A, Navarro L, Pardo-Seco J, Bello X, Pischedda S, Viz-Lasheras S, Camino-Mera A, Currás MJ, Ferreirós I, Mallah N, Rey-Vázquez S, Redondo L, Dacosta-Urbieta A, Caamaño-Viña F, Rivero-Calle I, Rodriguez-Tenreiro C, Martinón-Torres F, Salas A. · Scientific Reports · 2023 · Human before–after transcriptomic study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A music session was associated with blood gene-expression changes in people with age-related cognitive disorders and healthy controls. Some affected pathways overlapped inversely with disease-associated expression patterns.

Recorded limitations

Before–after molecular associations and cross-dataset comparisons do not establish that music reverses dementia or changes brain gene expression.
