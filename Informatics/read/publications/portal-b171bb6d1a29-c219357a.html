Transcendental Meditation versus exposure therapy in veterans with PTSD

The Lancet Psychiatry / U.S. DoD funded · 2018 · Anchor randomised controlled trial

Original publication

Recorded summary

In 203 veterans, Transcendental Meditation was non-inferior to prolonged exposure therapy for change in clinician-assessed PTSD symptoms at three months.

Recorded limitations

One trial of a structured 12-session practice; it does not test passive or sub-audible mantra delivery.
