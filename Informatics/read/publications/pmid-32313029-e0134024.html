Quantitative cellular-resolution map of the oxytocin receptor in postnatally developing mouse brains

Kyra T. Newmaster; Zachary T. Nolan; Uree Chon; Daniel J. Vanselow; Abigael R. Weit; Manal Tabbaa; Shizu Hidema; Katsuhiko Nishimori; Elizabeth A. D. Hammock; Yongsoo Kim · Nature Communications · 2020 · Experimental developmental brain-mapping study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Cellular-resolution maps show that oxytocin receptor expression changes across brain regions and postnatal development in mice. Reporter imaging and RNA measurements identify transient cortical expression and region-specific patterns, providing an anatomical reference for oxytocin research.

Recorded limitations

Mouse developmental maps do not establish the corresponding receptor distribution or functional connections in the human brain.
