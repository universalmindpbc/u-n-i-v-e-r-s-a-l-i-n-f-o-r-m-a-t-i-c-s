DNA methylation changes in association with trauma-focused psychotherapy efficacy in treatment-resistant depression patients: a prospective longitudinal study.

Carvalho Silva R, Martini P, Hohoff C, Mattevi S, Bortolomasi M, Menesello V, Gennarelli M, Baune BT, Minelli A. · European journal of psychotraumatology · 2024 · Exploratory DNA-methylation study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Blood DNA methylation was studied in 30 people with treatment-resistant depression receiving EMDR (18) or trauma-focused CBT (12). Differentially methylated regions included inflammation-related genes and pathways.

Recorded limitations

Small treatment groups and concurrent usual care limit attribution. DNA methylation is a separate measurement from RNA expression.
