40 Hz light flickering facilitates the glymphatic flow via adenosine signaling in mice

Sun X, Dias L, Peng C, Zhang Z, Ge H, Wang Z, Jin J, Jia M, Xu T, Guo W, Zheng W, He Y, Wu Y, Cai X, Agostinho P, Qu J, Cunha RA, Zhou X, Bai R, Chen JF. · Cell Discovery · 2024 · Controlled mouse visual-stimulation experiments

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

40 Hz flickering light increased glymphatic influx and efflux in mice. Genetic and pharmacological tests implicated adenosine A2A receptor signalling and aquaporin-4.

Recorded limitations

This measures physiological and signalling mechanisms in mice. It does not establish OXTR transcription changes or a safe, effective human visual protocol.
