Facilitating improvements in young people's social relationships to prevent or treat depression: A review of empirically supported interventions.

Filia K, Eastwood O, Herniman S, Badcock P. · Translational psychiatry · 2021 · Review / synthesis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Interpersonal approaches, especially IPT-A, have evidence for youth depression; preventive/social approaches are more variable.

Recorded limitations

Selective narrative synthesis from 140 eligible reports, not a new meta-analysis; clinical interpersonal therapy differs from ordinary group participation.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
