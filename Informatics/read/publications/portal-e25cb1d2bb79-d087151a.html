Theta binaural beats for pain, cognition and anxiety

EXPLORE · 2026 · Systematic review + meta-analysis of RCTs

Original publication

Recorded summary

Across 13 RCTs, theta (4-8 Hz) binaural beats reduced pain, showed anxiety benefit mainly in procedural and chronic-pain settings, and improved episodic memory—supporting focused testing in Universal Mind's 5-8 Hz range.

Recorded limitations

Overall pain evidence was low certainty, outcomes varied, and the review does not establish PTSD efficacy or validate a complete sound layer.
