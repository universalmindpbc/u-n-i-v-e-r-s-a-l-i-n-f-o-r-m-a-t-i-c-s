Efficacy of Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing in Children and Adolescent with Post-traumatic Stress Disorder: A Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials.

Moreno-Alcázar A, Treen D, Valiente-Gómez A, Sio-Eroles A, Pérez V, Amann BL, Radua J. · Frontiers in psychology · 2017 · Systematic review / meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

EMDR reduced PTSD and anxiety relative to inactive comparators; the depression result was not statistically significant. Comparisons with CBT did not establish a clear advantage.

Recorded limitations

Small older evidence base, variable bias and populations, and substantial overlap with later reviews; not an additional set of independent trials.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
