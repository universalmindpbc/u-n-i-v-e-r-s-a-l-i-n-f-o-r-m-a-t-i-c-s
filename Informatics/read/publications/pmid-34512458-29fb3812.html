Coronavirus Disease 2019 Emergency and Remote Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing Group Therapy With Adolescents and Young Adults: Overcoming Lockdown With the Butterfly Hug.

Lazzaroni E, Invernizzi R, Fogliato E, Pagani M, Maslovaric G. · Frontiers in Psychology · 2021 · Uncontrolled clinical cohort

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A remote clinician-led EMDR group intervention during COVID-19 was associated with reduced traumatic stress and anxiety in participants aged 13–24 who were already receiving mental-health care.

Recorded limitations

Mixed adolescent and young-adult sample with no randomized control. Findings do not isolate an 18–24 subgroup or unguided use.
