Use of Fibonacci numbers in lipidomics – Enumerating various classes of fatty acids

Stefan Schuster; Maximilian Fichtner; Severin Sasso · Scientific Reports · 2017 · Combinatorial proof and chemical enumeration

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The paper derives Fibonacci counts for a defined class of unbranched fatty-acid structures when cis/trans isomers are not distinguished. Modified assumptions produce generalized recurrences, including Pell-number cases.

Recorded limitations

The result counts chemically specified possibilities. It does not show that living organisms produce all counted structures, that measured lipid abundances follow the sequence, or that the rule applies to arbitrary molecules.
