Predicting psychosocial intervention response from baseline gene expression

Ricon-Becker I, West TN, Fredrickson BL, Kaplan DM, Mehl MR, Raison CL, Cole SW. · Brain, behavior, and immunity · 2024 · Prediction analyses across two randomized trials

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Baseline CREB activity predicted selected psychological responses to compassion-based meditation in two datasets. The same relationship was not seen for mindfulness meditation.

Recorded limitations

The study concerns prediction from baseline expression rather than a demonstrated therapy-induced transcriptional mechanism. Clinical deployment requires further validation.
