Brainwave entrainment to minimise sedative drug doses in paediatric surgery: a randomised controlled trial.

Schmid W, Marhofer P, Opfermann P, Zadrazil M, Kimberger O, Triffterer L, Marhofer D, Klug W. · British journal of anaesthesia · 2020 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 49 boys undergoing surgery with a caudal block, audiovisual brainwave entrainment reduced the median propofol infusion requirement from 4.2 to 3.0 mg/kg/hour.

Recorded limitations

The intervention combined sound and visual stimulation and cannot establish an isolated binaural-beat effect. Findings concern supervised paediatric anaesthesia, not unsupervised sedation or general wellbeing.
