Changes in the expression of inflammatory and epigenetic-modulatory genes after an intensive meditation retreat.

Álvarez-López MJ, Conklin QA, Cosín-Tomás M, Shields GS, King BG, Zanesco AP, Kaliman P, Saron CD. · Comprehensive psychoneuroendocrinology · 2022 · Nonrandomized controlled transcriptomic study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A three-week retreat study comparing 28 experienced meditators with 34 community-based practitioners reported changes in TNF and chromatin-related blood gene-expression patterns.

Recorded limitations

Retreat participation was not randomised. Lifestyle, setting and participant selection remain plausible explanations; molecular changes do not demonstrate improved disease outcomes.
