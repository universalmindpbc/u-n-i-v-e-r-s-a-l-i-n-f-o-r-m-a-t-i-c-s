Binaural beat stimulation as a tool to reduce preoperative anxiety in patients undergoing elective surgery and general anesthesia - results from a randomized controlled trial.

Ostertag J, Zinn S, Georgii MT, Niederle MB, Pinto Campos C, Schneider G, Hayden O, Kratzer S, Kreuzer M. · Biomedical physics & engineering express · 2025 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Preoperative binaural-beat stimulation produced a greater reduction in reported state anxiety than the control condition. The reported difference in relative anxiety change was approximately six percentage points.

Recorded limitations

The authors identified music and relaxation as plausible contributors. Alpha power did not differ between groups; the trial does not isolate a beat-specific mechanism.
