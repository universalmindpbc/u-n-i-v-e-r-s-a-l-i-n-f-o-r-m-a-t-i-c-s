Low-Intensity Online Intervention for Mental Distress Among Help-Seeking Young People in Hong Kong: A Randomized Clinical Trial.

Suen YN, Hui CLM, Lei LKS, Leung CM, Wong SMY, Lai GCH, Chau EHS, Wong MTH, Chan SKW, Wong GHY, Chen EYH. · JAMA network open · 2025 · Randomized controlled trial report · double blinding not verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Primary distress, depression and anxiety outcomes null; some secondary stress/resilience signals disappeared under multiple-imputation analysis.

Recorded limitations

Missing data sensitivity, short follow-up and multiple outcomes; cannot present secondary signals as robust efficacy.
