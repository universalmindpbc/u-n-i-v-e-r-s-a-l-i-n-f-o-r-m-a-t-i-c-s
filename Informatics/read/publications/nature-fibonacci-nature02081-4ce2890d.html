Regulation of phyllotaxis by polar auxin transport

Didier Reinhardt; Eva-Rachele Pesce; Pia Stieger; Therese Mandel; Kurt Baltensperger; Malcolm Bennett; Jan Traas; Jiří Friml; Cris Kuhlemeier · Nature · 2003 · Plant developmental experiments and mechanistic model

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Experiments link polar auxin transport to the positions of new plant organs. Existing primordia act as auxin sinks, helping define the spacing and stability of phyllotactic patterns.

Recorded limitations

This is mechanistic context for plant pattern formation. It does not establish a universal Fibonacci mechanism across organisms or molecular systems; appraisal here is limited to the abstract and publisher metadata.
