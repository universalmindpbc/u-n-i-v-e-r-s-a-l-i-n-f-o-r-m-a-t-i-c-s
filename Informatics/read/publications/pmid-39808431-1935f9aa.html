Digital Meditation to Target Employee Stress: A Randomized Clinical Trial.

Radin RM, Vacarro J, Fromer E, Ahmadi SE, Guan JY, Fisher SM, Pressman SD, Hunter JF, Sweeny K, Tomiyama AJ, Hofschneider LT, Zawadzki MJ, Gavrilova L, Epel ES, Prather AA. · JAMA network open · 2025 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 1,458 employees, an eight-week digital meditation programme improved perceived stress relative to waitlist, with an effect size of 0.85 at eight weeks and 0.71 at four months.

Recorded limitations

Participants volunteered, most were women, and the comparator did not match attention. Greater use correlated with improvement but was not itself randomised.
