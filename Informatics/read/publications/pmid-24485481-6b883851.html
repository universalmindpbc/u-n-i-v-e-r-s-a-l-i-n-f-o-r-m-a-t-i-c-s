Rapid changes in histone deacetylases and inflammatory gene expression in expert meditators

Kaliman P, Alvarez-López MJ, Cosín-Tomás M, Rosenkranz MA, Lutz A, Davidson RJ. · Psychoneuroendocrinology · 2014 · Nonrandomized controlled study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

After practice, meditators showed lower expression of HDAC2, HDAC3, HDAC9, RIPK2 and PTGS2 (COX-2), alongside changes in histone modification. RIPK2 and HDAC2 expression were associated with faster cortisol recovery.

Recorded limitations

Groups differed in prior meditation experience and were not randomized. Peripheral blood findings do not establish equivalent changes in brain tissue.
