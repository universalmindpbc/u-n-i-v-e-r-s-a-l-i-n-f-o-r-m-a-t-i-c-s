Fast and simple high-capacity quantum cryptography with error detection

Hong Lai; Ming-Xing Luo; Josef Pieprzyk; Jun Zhang; Lei Pan; Shudong Li; Mehmet A. Orgun · Scientific Reports · 2017 · Theoretical quantum-cryptography algorithm

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Fibonacci- and Lucas-valued orbital-angular-momentum states supply inputs to recursive matrix-based coding. The paper proposes matrix operations for high-capacity key use and error detection.

Recorded limitations

Claims depend on the stated protocol assumptions. A proposal and analytical examples do not establish a production-ready cryptosystem, end-to-end hardware performance, or independent cryptanalytic validation.
