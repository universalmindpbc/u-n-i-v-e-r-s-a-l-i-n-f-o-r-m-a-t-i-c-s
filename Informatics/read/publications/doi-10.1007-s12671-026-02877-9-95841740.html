Dynamic Methylation Changes in Alzheimer’s Disease-Related Genes During Mindfulness Practice – a Proof-of-Concept Study

Mindfulness · 2026 · Nonrandomized controlled pre/post study

Original publication

Recorded summary

In 17 long-term meditators and 17 matched controls, researchers examined methylation at 19 CpG sites across seven genes. A month-long retreat was associated with changes at selected sites, including ABCA7 and HOXA3; some ADAM10 differences separated meditators from controls.

Recorded limitations

A small, nonrandomized blood-methylation study. It did not measure brain RNA or establish prevention or treatment of Alzheimer’s disease. Baseline average ADAM10 methylation did not remain significant after multiple-testing correction.
