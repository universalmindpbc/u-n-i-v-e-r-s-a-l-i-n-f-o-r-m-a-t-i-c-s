Effectiveness of Mantra-Based Meditation on Mental Health: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

Álvarez-Pérez Y, Rivero-Santana A, Perestelo-Pérez L, Duarte-Díaz A, Ramos-García V, Toledo-Chávarri A, Torres-Castaño A, León-Salas B, Infante-Ventura D, González-Hernández N, Rodríguez-Rodríguez L, Serrano-Aguilar P. · International journal of environmental research and public health · 2022 · Systematic review + meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

RCT evidence showed small-to-moderate reductions in anxiety, depression, stress and post-traumatic stress for mantra-based meditation.

Recorded limitations

The review found risk of bias and too few psychiatric samples or long-term follow-ups.
