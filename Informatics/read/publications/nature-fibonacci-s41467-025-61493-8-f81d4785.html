Realizing string-net condensation: Fibonacci anyon braiding for universal gates and sampling chromatic polynomials

Zlatko K. Minev; Khadijeh Najafi; Swarnadeep Majumder; Juven Wang; Ady Stern; Eun-Ah Kim; Chao-Ming Jian; Guanyu Zhu · Nature Communications · 2025 · Quantum-circuit design and superconducting-processor experiments

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The authors construct Fibonacci string-net states with a dynamical preparation method, measure and braid anyons, and estimate chromatic polynomials for several graphs using quantum samples.

Recorded limitations

The experiments are proof of principle with error mitigation. The authors identify noise and comparison with classical approximation algorithms as unresolved barriers to a demonstrated quantum advantage.
