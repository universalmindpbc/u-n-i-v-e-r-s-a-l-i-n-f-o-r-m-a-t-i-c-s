Towards an understanding of spiral patterning in the Sargassum muticum shoot apex

Marina Linardić; Siobhan A. Braybrook · Scientific Reports · 2017 · Algal developmental observations and perturbation experiments

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Sargassum muticum displays a mean divergence angle close to the golden angle. Organ placement is uncoupled from apical-cell division orientation, while the tested auxin and wall-softening explanations fail to account for bud position.

Recorded limitations

The developmental signal remains unidentified. Similar geometry in algae and plants does not establish a shared molecular mechanism.
