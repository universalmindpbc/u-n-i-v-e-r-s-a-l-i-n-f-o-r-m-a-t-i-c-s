Sensogenomics of music and Alzheimer's disease: An interdisciplinary view from neuroscience, transcriptomics, and epigenomics

Navarro L, Gómez-Carballa A, Pischedda S, Montoto-Louzao J, Viz-Lasheras S, Camino-Mera A, Hinault T, Martinón-Torres F, Salas A. · Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience · 2023 · Literature synthesis and secondary omics analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The authors compared music-related genes with Alzheimer disease datasets and identified overlapping pathways and candidate genes, including SLC6A4.

Recorded limitations

This is an integrative analysis of earlier evidence. It does not show that music directly changes SLC6A4 expression in a clinical experiment.
