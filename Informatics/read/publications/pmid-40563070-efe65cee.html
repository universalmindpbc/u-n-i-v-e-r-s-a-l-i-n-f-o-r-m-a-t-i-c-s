Effects of preoperative binaural beats on the propofol dose for induction of general anesthesia: a randomized controlled trial.

Kim YJ, Choe HW, Yoon SB, Lee H, Kim HS, Seo JH. · Canadian journal of anaesthesia = Journal canadien d'anesthesie · 2025 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Among 69 surgical patients, 20 minutes of 1 Hz binaural beats reduced the mean propofol dose needed to stop responding to verbal commands from 105 to 87 mg; the difference was -18 mg, with a 95% confidence interval of -32 to -5 mg.

Recorded limitations

Anxiety and frontal EEG bands did not differ significantly. This supports an anaesthetic-dose finding within the tested clinical protocol, rather than a general claim of brainwave entrainment.
