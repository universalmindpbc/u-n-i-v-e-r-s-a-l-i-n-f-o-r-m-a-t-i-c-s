Transcendental Meditation practitioners show reduced expression of the Conserved Transcriptional Response to Adversity

Walton KG, Wenuganen S, Cole SW. · Brain, behavior, & immunity - health · 2023 · Observational comparison; secondary analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Long-term TM practitioners had lower expression of the prespecified CTRA gene set, with inferred reductions in NF-κB and AP-1 activity and higher interferon-response activity.

Recorded limitations

A very small observational comparison and secondary analysis of existing data. It cannot establish causation or separate meditation from long-term lifestyle differences.
