Addressing mental health need after COVID-19: a systematic review of remote EMDR therapy studies as an emerging option.

Kaptan SK, Kaya ZM, Akan A. · Frontiers in Psychiatry · 2024 · Systematic review

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Sixteen articles with 1,231 participants cover remote individual, group and computerised interventions.

Recorded limitations

Heterogeneous designs include uncontrolled studies; this is not a randomised comparison of delivery methods.
