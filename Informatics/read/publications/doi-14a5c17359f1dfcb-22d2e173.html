Transcriptional Modulation of Stress-Related Genes in Association with Early Life Stress Exposure and Trauma-Focused Psychotherapy in Treatment-Resistant Depression Patients

Rosana Carvalho Silva; Vincenzo Dattilo; Giulia Perusi; Monica Mazzelli; Elisabetta Maffioletti; Roberta Bazzanella; Marco Bortolomasi; Annamaria Cattaneo; Massimo Gennarelli; Alessandra Minelli · Journal of EMDR Practice and Research · 2023 · Longitudinal gene-expression study

Original publication

Recorded summary

In 21 people receiving EMDR or trauma-focused CBT for treatment-resistant depression, whole-blood NR3C1, FKBP5 and SGK1 expression varied over time. FKBP5 and SGK1 fell significantly at eight weeks; transcript levels rebounded during follow-up.

Recorded limitations

EMDR and CBT participants were analysed together, alongside medication. The 2021 microRNA paper uses the same underlying cohort; this is not an independent replication or evidence of an isolated eye-movement effect.
