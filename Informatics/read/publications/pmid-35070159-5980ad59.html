Inflammation-related microRNAs are involved in stressful life events exposure and in trauma-focused psychotherapy in treatment-resistant depressed patients.

Maffioletti E, Bocchio-Chiavetto L, Perusi G, Carvalho Silva R, Sacco C, Bazzanella R, Zampieri E, Bortolomasi M, Gennarelli M, Minelli A. · European journal of psychotraumatology · 2021 · Longitudinal molecular study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Blood microRNA was measured in 41 people with treatment-resistant depression; 21 received EMDR or trauma-focused CBT. miR-15a, miR-29a, miR-126, miR-195 and let-7f changed during treatment; baseline miR-29a was associated with response.

Recorded limitations

The longitudinal group combined 12 EMDR and nine CBT participants. This cohort also informs the 2023 stress-gene paper, so the two publications are not independent replications.
