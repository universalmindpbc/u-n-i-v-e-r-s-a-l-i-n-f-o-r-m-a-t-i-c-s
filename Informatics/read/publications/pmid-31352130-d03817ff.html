Pretreatment cortisol predicts trauma-focused psychotherapy response in youth with (partial) posttraumatic stress disorder.

Zantvoord JB, Ensink JBM, Op den Kelder R, Wessel AMA, Lok A, Lindauer RJL. · Psychoneuroendocrinology · 2019 · Prospective biomarker study

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Among 53 young people aged 8–18 treated with EMDR or trauma-focused CBT, higher pretreatment basal salivary cortisol was associated with greater subsequent symptom improvement.

Recorded limitations

The trauma script rarely produced a cortisol stress response. The predictive contribution was limited and insufficient for clinical application; this is not a gene-expression measurement.
