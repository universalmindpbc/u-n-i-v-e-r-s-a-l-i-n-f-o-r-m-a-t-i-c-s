The IBM Quantum heavy hex lattice

Paul Nation; Hanhee Paik; Andrew Cross; Zaira Nazario · IBM Quantum technical blog · 2021 · Hardware architecture explanation

Original publication

Recorded summary

IBM explains why its heavy-hex architecture uses a sparse pattern of superconducting-qubit connections. The design reduces frequency collisions and unwanted effects on neighbouring qubits while balancing connectivity and the operations required by a calculation.

Recorded limitations

This is a vendor technical explanation, not a peer-reviewed Fibonacci study. Its benefits depend on superconducting hardware and control choices; the shape alone is not evidence of universal quantum stability.
