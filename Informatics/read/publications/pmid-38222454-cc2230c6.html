Light damage induces inflammatory factors in mouse retina and vitreous humor

Liu W, Zhu X, Ge X, Chen Y, Li DW, Gong L. · Molecular vision · 2023 · Mouse light-induced retinal injury model

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Strong light exposure induced retinal inflammatory transcriptional changes. Vitreous IL-6 protein was approximately tenfold higher in the injury model.

Recorded limitations

The IL-6 fold change concerns vitreous protein. This is a harmful exposure model, not evidence for a therapeutic visual protocol.
