Apprentices' Attitudes Toward Using a Mental Health Mobile App to Support Healthy Coping: Mixed Methods Study.

Choi I, Petrie K, Einboden R, Collins D, Ryan R, Johnston D, Harvey SB, Glozier N, Wray A, Deady M. · JMIR human factors · 2022 · Qualitative study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Only 11% used a wellbeing app; 87% interested in helping friends and 78% in self-help skills. Social connection, enjoyable activities, cognitive and self-care strategies were valued.

Recorded limitations

Small mainly male Sydney sample with adults over target; stated interest is not uptake or efficacy.
