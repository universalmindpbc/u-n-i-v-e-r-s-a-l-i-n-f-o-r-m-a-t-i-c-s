Clinical and Cost-Effectiveness of Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing for Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder in Children and Adolescents: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.

Sutton A, Carroll C, Simpson E, Forsyth J, Rayner A, Ren S, Franklin M, Wood E. · Clinical psychology & psychotherapy · 2025 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Eight clinical RCTs include 794 children and adolescents; one additional study examines cost-effectiveness.

Recorded limitations

Most comparisons use waitlist or usual care; limited active-comparator and economic evidence. EMDR UK funding is disclosed.
