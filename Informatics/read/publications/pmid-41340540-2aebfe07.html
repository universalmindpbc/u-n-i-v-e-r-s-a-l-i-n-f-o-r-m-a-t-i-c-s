Clinical and Cost-Effectiveness of Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing for Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder in Children and Adolescents: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.

Sutton A, Carroll C, Simpson E, Forsyth J, Rayner A, Ren S, Franklin M, Wood E. · Clinical psychology & psychotherapy · 2025 · Systematic review / meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Pooled PTSD symptom effects favoured EMDR over waiting/usual care (SMD 1.57, 95% credible interval 0.07–3.21). Two direct comparisons did not establish superiority over trauma-focused CBT. Economic evidence was sparse.

Recorded limitations

All clinical studies were judged moderate or high risk of bias; wide uncertainty and little long-term evidence. Reviews and their included trials are not independent corroborating samples.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
