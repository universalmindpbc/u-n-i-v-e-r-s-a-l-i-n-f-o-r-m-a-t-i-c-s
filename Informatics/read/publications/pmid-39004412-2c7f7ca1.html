Brain wave modulation and EEG power changes during auditory beats stimulation

Dos Anjos T, Di Rienzo F, Benoit CE, Daligault S, Guillot A. · Neuroscience · 2024 · Comparative EEG study

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 28 healthy participants, four minutes of isochronic-derived stimulation produced greater gamma-band EEG power changes than gamma binaural beats and white noise, alongside a distinct time-related alpha response.

Recorded limitations

The stimulus alternated audible frequencies rather than using a conventional fixed-carrier isochronic tone and did not test Universal Mind's exact protocol. It measured short-term EEG responses, not a clinical outcome.
