Eye movement desensitization and reprocessing therapy in subsyndromal bipolar patients with a history of traumatic events: a randomized, controlled pilot-study.

Novo P, Landin-Romero R, Radua J, Vicens V, Fernandez I, Garcia F, Pomarol-Clotet E, McKenna PJ, Shapiro F, Amann BL. · Psychiatry research · 2014 · Single-blind randomized controlled pilot trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 20 people with bipolar disorder and trauma histories, EMDR improved depressive, hypomanic and trauma symptoms relative to usual care after treatment, using blinded outcome ratings.

Recorded limitations

The trial was small, with unequal dropout between groups. Most differences were not sustained at the 24-week assessment; this is an adjunctive pilot rather than evidence for replacing established bipolar treatment.
