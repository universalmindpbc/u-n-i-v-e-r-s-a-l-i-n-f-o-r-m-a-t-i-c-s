Effect of a Web-Based Heartfulness Program on the Mental Well-Being, Biomarkers, and Gene Expression Profile of Health Care Students: Randomized Controlled Trial

Thimmapuram J, Patel KD, Bhatt D, Chauhan A, Madhusudhan D, Bhatt KK, Deshpande S, Budhbhatti U, Joshi C. · JMIR bioinformatics and biotechnology · 2024 · Randomized controlled trial; blinding not reported

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

At 12 weeks, RNA sequencing identified 292 upregulated and 1,180 downregulated genes in the meditation group relative to controls. Reported stress and distress improved. IL-6 protein fell in both groups, with a larger reduction in controls.

Recorded limitations

An exploratory trial with an inactive comparator and exclusion of participants missing follow-up. Enrichment analysis does not establish changes in every gene belonging to a pathway.
