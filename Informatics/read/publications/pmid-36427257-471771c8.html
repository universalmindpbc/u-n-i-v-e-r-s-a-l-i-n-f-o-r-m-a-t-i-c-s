Mantram repetition and psychological distress: A systematic review and meta-analysis

Schneider JK, Abdullahi SG, Easton SD, Willis DG. · Journal of psychiatric and mental health nursing · 2023 · Systematic review + meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Across the eligible comparison studies, mantram repetition produced a small pooled reduction in psychological distress.

Recorded limitations

Only four of 12 retrieved studies measured distress with comparison groups.
