Immediate effects of binaural beat stimulation on prefrontal cortical activation after cycling exercise: an electroencephalography-based study.

Hsiao SF, Liu TL, Lin YC, Yen CW, Siu KC, Lin JH. · Experimental brain research · 2026 · Randomized crossover experiment

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 52 completers, theta-range binaural stimulation with pink noise after cycling increased prefrontal theta and alpha EEG power relative to sham stimulation.

Recorded limitations

This was an immediate physiological experiment in healthy adults. It did not establish improved recovery, cognition or a clinical treatment effect; 60 people were recruited.
