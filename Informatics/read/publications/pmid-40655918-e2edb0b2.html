Music interventions for posttraumatic stress disorder: A systematic review

Wang CC, Emrich M, Rives H, Ovalles A, Wright D, Wyka K, Difede J. · Journal of mood and anxiety disorders · 2024 · Systematic review

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Fourteen studies were included; 13 reported decreased PTSD symptoms, and passive-listening studies also showed promise.

Recorded limitations

Nine studies were feasibility studies; small samples and weak control groups limit certainty.
