The role of school connectedness in the prevention of youth depression and anxiety: a systematic review with youth consultation.

Raniti M, Rakesh D, Patton GC, Sawyer SM. · BMC public health · 2022 · Review / synthesis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

School connectedness usually predicted fewer later depressive/anxiety symptoms; two interventions improved depression.

Recorded limitations

Predominantly observational US evidence, limited diagnostic/remission outcomes and few interventions; an independent academy differs from school.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
