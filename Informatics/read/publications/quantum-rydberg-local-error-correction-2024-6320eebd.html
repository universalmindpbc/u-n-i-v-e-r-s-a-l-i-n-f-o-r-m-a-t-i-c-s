Enhancing detection of topological order by local error correction

Iris Cong; Nishad Maskara; Minh C. Tran; Hannes Pichler; Giulia Semeghini; Susanne F. Yelin; Soonwon Choi; Mikhail D. Lukin · Nature Communications · 2024 · Numerical modelling and analysis of Rydberg-atom measurements

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Cong and colleagues use local error-correction ideas to identify topological order despite noise. They test the method numerically and apply it to measurements from a Rydberg-atom quantum spin liquid, making it directly relevant to analysing neutral-atom experiments.

Recorded limitations

The method improves the diagnosis of a prepared state. It is not a demonstration that Aquila stores arbitrary information fault-tolerantly, and it does not test M9 P24 sequences.
