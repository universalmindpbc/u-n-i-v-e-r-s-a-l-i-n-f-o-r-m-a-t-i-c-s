Trauma-focused treatments for refugee children and adolescents with PTSD: a three-arm randomized controlled trial comparing the efficacy of EMDR and KIDNET versus a waitlist control group (the KIEM study).

Velu ME, de Roos C, van der Aa N, Jongedijk R, Lindauer R, Mooren T. · Child and adolescent psychiatry and mental health · 2026 · Randomized controlled trial · single-blind (assessor)

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Ninety-six refugee children aged 8–18 were allocated to EMDR, KIDNET or waitlist; both active treatments improved PTSD outcomes.

Recorded limitations

Small arms and different dropout rates limit comparative claims. Outcomes do not establish treatment equivalence.
