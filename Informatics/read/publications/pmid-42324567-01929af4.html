Trauma-focused treatments for refugee children and adolescents with PTSD: a three-arm randomized controlled trial comparing the efficacy of EMDR and KIDNET versus a waitlist control group (the KIEM study).

Velu ME, de Roos C, van der Aa N, Jongedijk R, Lindauer R, Mooren T. · Child and adolescent psychiatry and mental health · 2026 · Randomized controlled trial report · double blinding not verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Both treatments reduced clinician-rated PTSD more than waiting (EMDR d=1.31; KIDNET d=0.94). EMDR had a modest advantage on clinician-rated severity over KIDNET, without consistent superiority on other outcomes. Parent-reported results differed from child/clinician reports.

Recorded limitations

Younger mixed sample, treatment-versus-wait timing differences, incomplete assessor masking and short follow-up; after re-randomization dropout was 20.8% EMDR and 10.9% KIDNET.
