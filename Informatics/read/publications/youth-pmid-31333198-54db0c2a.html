Digital Mental Health Interventions for Depression, Anxiety, and Enhancement of Psychological Well-Being Among College Students: Systematic Review.

Lattie EG, Adkins EC, Winquist N, Stiles-Shields C, Wafford QE, Graham AK. · Journal of medical Internet research · 2019 · Review / synthesis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Many digital interventions showed some symptom benefit, but only four studies examined broad campus implementation and half omitted usability outcomes.

Recorded limitations

Moderate-to-severe bias common, heterogeneous designs and vote-counted results; publication counts do not equal independent interventions.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
