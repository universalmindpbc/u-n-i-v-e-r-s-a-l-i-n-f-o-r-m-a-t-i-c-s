Enhancing image security via chaotic maps, Fibonacci, Tribonacci transformations, and DWT diffusion: a robust data encryption approach

Mohammad Mazyad Hazzazi; Mujeeb Ur Rehman; Arslan Shafique; Amer Aljaedi; Zaid Bassfar; Aminu Bello Usman · Scientific Reports · 2024 · Image-encryption algorithm with numerical tests

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The proposed grayscale-image encryption pipeline combines chaotic keys, Fibonacci pixel permutation, Tribonacci transformations, bit-plane operations and wavelet-based diffusion. The paper reports statistical and timing tests.

Recorded limitations

Entropy, pixel correlation and timing tests do not alone establish cryptographic security. The multi-component pipeline also prevents attributing its reported performance solely to Fibonacci transforms.
