Topology-driven quantum phase transitions in time-reversal-invariant anyonic quantum liquids

Charlotte Gils; Simon Trebst; Alexei Kitaev; Andreas W. W. Ludwig; Matthias Troyer; Zhenghan Wang · Nature Physics · 2009 · Analytical and numerical theory of interacting anyons

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

An interacting-anyon model connects topological quantum phases through topology-driven transitions. Fibonacci-anyon theory provides a concrete setting, including an exactly solvable linear geometry.

Recorded limitations

This is theoretical work rather than a device demonstration. Applicability to a physical implementation requires separate evidence; the present review uses the abstract and accessible figure descriptions.
