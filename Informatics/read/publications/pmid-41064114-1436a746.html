Music elicits different gene expression responses in the buccal cavity of age-related cognitive disorders patients and healthy controls

Gómez-Carballa A, Navarro L, Mallah NEZ, Bello X, Pischedda S, Viz-Lasheras S, Currás MJ, Ferreirós-Vidal I, Mallah N, Montoto-Louzao J, Camino-Mera A, Castelo-Martínez L, Rey-Vázquez S, Redondo L, Dacosta-Urbieta A, Rivero-Calle I, Rodríguez-Tenreiro C, Martinón-Torres F, Salas A. · Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience · 2025 · Human before–after targeted transcriptomic study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Brief music exposure was associated with differing buccal expression responses in cognitive-disorder patients and controls. Highlighted genes included LGALS3, CXCL8 and THOP1.

Recorded limitations

Peripheral tissue and exploratory comparisons; the results do not establish changes in brain tissue or therapeutic efficacy.
