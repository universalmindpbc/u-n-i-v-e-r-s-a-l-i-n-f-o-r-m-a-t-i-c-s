Dopamine receptor signaling regulates fibrotic activation of retinal pigmented epithelial cells

Gao AY, Link PA, Bakri SJ, Haak AJ. · American journal of physiology. Cell physiology · 2022 · Human retinal pigment epithelial cell-line experiment

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

DRD2 and DRD5 were prominent receptors in ARPE-19 cells. Dopamine-receptor manipulation altered profibrotic gene expression and cellular fibrosis measures.

Recorded limitations

Pharmacological receptor manipulation provides ocular pathway context. It is not a test of light or visual-stimulation effects on DRD2 expression.
