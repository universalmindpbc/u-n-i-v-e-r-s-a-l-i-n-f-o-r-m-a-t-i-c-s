Introducing a mindfulness-based intervention in school curriculum to 16-24-year-olds. A nationwide cluster-randomized trial.

Juul L, Frydenberg M, Bonde EH, Beck MS, Goetzsche K, Bruggisser S, Fjorback LO. · Npj mental health research · 2025 · Randomized controlled trial report · double blinding not verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Primary wellbeing outcome did not improve overall at three or six months. Small female secondary-school subgroup signal at three months was not sustained.

Recorded limitations

School/student attrition, self-report and subgroup multiplicity. Registration occurred during the 2019–2021 study. Teacher reports of no intervention-related events do not prove absence of harms.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
