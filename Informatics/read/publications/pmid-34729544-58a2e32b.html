Randomized controlled trial of stress reduction with meditation and health education in black men and women with high normal and normal blood pressure.

Schneider RH, Grim C, Kotchen T, Marwaha K, Kotchen J, Salerno JW, King CG, Nidich S, Alexander CN. · American journal of preventive cardiology · 2021 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Among 304 Black adults stratified by baseline blood pressure, meditation reduced systolic pressure by approximately 3.3 mmHg relative to health education in the high-normal-pressure group.

Recorded limitations

The benefit did not extend to the normal-pressure group or diastolic pressure. Hypertension-event differences were not significant, so the result is subgroup- and endpoint-specific.
