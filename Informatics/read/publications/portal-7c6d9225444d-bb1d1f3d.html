A multisite retrospective review exploring the delivery of eye movement desensitization and reprocessing (EMDR) therapy to veterans via telehealth (TH) versus in person (IP).

Fairbanks CLD, Penix-Smith EA, Glitsos SC, Keener KD, Giorgio JM, Poulos KH, Albinson LF, Baker CE, McGuirl CA, Wisniewski SP. · Psychological trauma : theory, research, practice and policy · 2025 · Multisite retrospective review

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Across 256 veterans, both delivery groups improved. Completion, reliable change and clinically significant change did not differ, although the in-person symptom reduction was larger.

Recorded limitations

Treatment format was chosen rather than randomised. This supports individual telehealth delivery; it does not test online group EMDR.
