Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Help-seeking intentions and literacy improved; actual help seeking, self-stigma, depression and anxiety did not significantly improve.

Recorded limitations

Selected internet-connected population, short follow-up and cultural setting; intentions are not behaviour.
