Neural mechanisms of mindfulness: reduced neural reactivity to social-evaluative threat accounts for mindfulness intervention effects on inflammatory gene expression

Blades RB, Boyle CC, Eisenberger NI, Cole SW, Korecki JRT, Fuligni AJ, Bower JE. · Brain, behavior, and immunity · 2026 · Randomized controlled trial; registered single-blind (outcome assessors)

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

After six weeks, the meditation group showed lower neural responses to social-evaluative threat and lower inferred NF-κB activity. The gene-regulation difference lost statistical significance after adjustment for the neural-response change.

Recorded limitations

A small waitlist-controlled trial. Promoter analysis estimates transcription-factor activity; the analysis does not establish a causal neural mechanism.
