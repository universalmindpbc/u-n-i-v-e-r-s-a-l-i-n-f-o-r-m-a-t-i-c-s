The effect of alpha binaural beat music on orthodontic pain after initial archwire placement: A randomized controlled trial.

Aly AE, Hansa I, Ferguson DJ, Vaid NR. · Dental press journal of orthodontics · 2023 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 60 orthodontic patients, alpha binaural-beat music reduced several pain measures late in the first week after appliance placement relative to no music.

Recorded limitations

Pain did not differ between binaural-beat music and otherwise similar music without beats. The successful audio experience does not establish an additional beat-specific benefit.
