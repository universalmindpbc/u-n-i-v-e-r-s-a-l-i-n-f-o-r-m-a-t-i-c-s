Large-scale genomic study reveals robust activation of the immune system following advanced Inner Engineering meditation retreat.

Chandran V, Bermúdez ML, Koka M, Chandran B, Pawale D, Vishnubhotla R, Alankar S, Maturi R, Subramaniam B, Sadhasivam S. · Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America · 2021 · Uncontrolled longitudinal transcriptomic study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

An eight-day meditation retreat study reported coordinated blood-transcriptome changes in immune-related genes, including interferon-associated pathways, providing a candidate molecular signature for further testing.

Recorded limitations

The intensive retreat lacked a randomised control and combined multiple lifestyle changes. Expression changes do not establish improved antiviral protection or clinical treatment efficacy.
