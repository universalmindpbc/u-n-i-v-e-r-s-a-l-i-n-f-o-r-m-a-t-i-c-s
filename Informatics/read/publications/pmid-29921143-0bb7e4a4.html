Individual Treatment of Posttraumatic Stress Disorder Using Mantram Repetition: A Randomized Clinical Trial.

Bormann JE, Thorp SR, Smith E, Glickman M, Beck D, Plumb D, Zhao S, Ackland PE, Rodgers CS, Heppner P, Herz LR, Elwy AR. · The American journal of psychiatry · 2018 · Single-blind randomized controlled trial (outcome assessors blinded)

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A two-site trial in 173 veterans found greater clinician-rated PTSD improvement with an eight-week mantram programme than present-centred therapy; the between-group CAPS difference was approximately ten points. Insomnia also improved.

Recorded limitations

Participants knew the intervention received. Not every responder measure differed, and results apply to taught mantram practice rather than passive or sub-audible delivery.
