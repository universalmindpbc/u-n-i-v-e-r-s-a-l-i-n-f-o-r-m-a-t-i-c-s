Emergence of criticality through a cascade of delocalization transitions in quasiperiodic chains

V. Goblot; A. Štrkalj; N. Pernet; J. L. Lado; C. Dorow; A. Lemaître; L. Le Gratiet; A. Harouri; I. Sagnes; S. Ravets; A. Amo; J. Bloch; O. Zilberberg · Nature Physics · 2020 · Theoretical modelling and cavity-polariton experiment

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Interpolating between Aubry–André and Fibonacci chains produces a predicted cascade of band-dependent delocalization transitions. Cavity-polariton measurements detect the first transition and clarify its microscopic origin.

Recorded limitations

The experimental evidence covers the first transition in a specific platform. The complete cascade and wider device applications require further investigation.
