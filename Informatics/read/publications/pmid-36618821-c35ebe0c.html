The oxytocin receptor represents a key hub in the GPCR heteroreceptor network: potential relevance for brain and behavior

Borroto-Escuela DO, Cuesta-Marti C, Lopez-Salas A, Chruścicka-Smaga B, Crespo-Ramírez M, Tesoro-Cruz E, Palacios-Lagunas DA, Perez de la Mora M, Schellekens H, Fuxe K. · Frontiers in Molecular Neuroscience · 2022 · Narrative review of receptor-complex studies

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The review describes OXTR-containing receptor complexes, including dopamine D2 receptor–OXTR complexes reported in rat striatal regions, and discusses potential functional consequences for behaviour.

Recorded limitations

Receptor complex formation and signalling do not establish human DRD2 transcriptional regulation. This review does not test sensory interventions, the Fibonacci hypothesis or all proposed target relationships.
