A Web-Based Adolescent Positive Psychology Program in Schools: Randomized Controlled Trial.

Burckhardt R, Manicavasagar V, Batterham PJ, Miller LM, Talbot E, Lum A. · Journal of medical Internet research · 2015 · Randomized controlled trial report · double blinding not verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

No superiority for depression, stress or overall symptoms; control alone improved flourishing. Both groups improved some outcomes.

Recorded limitations

Four high-socioeconomic schools, variable duration/fidelity and unblinded self-report; same programme/registry as community trial but distinct school cohort.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
