Effectiveness of brainwave entrainment on pre-operative fear and anxiety in pediatric dental patients: a randomized controlled trial.

Shehani F, Samuel V, Kavitha R, Mani R. · European archives of paediatric dentistry : official journal of the European Academy of Paediatric Dentistry · 2024 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A 252-child dental trial reported reduced preoperative fear, anxiety and physiological arousal with device-based brainwave entrainment compared with standard behaviour management.

Recorded limitations

The device combines stimulation components. The abstract does not establish a binaural-beat-only effect, and masking, allocation and registration timing require full-text appraisal.
