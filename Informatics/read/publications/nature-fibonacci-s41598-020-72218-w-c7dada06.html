The architecture of starch blocklets follows phyllotaxic rules

Francesco Spinozzi; Claudio Ferrero; Serge Perez · Scientific Reports · 2020 · Geometric macromolecular model with scattering comparisons

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The authors construct golden-angle spiral ellipsoids from starch-related structural units and calculate scattering patterns. Similarities to reported blocklet features motivate a proposed amylopectin architecture.

Recorded limitations

The golden-angle arrangement is imposed in the construction. Agreement with selected structural and scattering features does not uniquely identify the native architecture or establish a biosynthetic mechanism.
