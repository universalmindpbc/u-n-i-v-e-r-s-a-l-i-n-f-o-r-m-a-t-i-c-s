The Effect of Binaural Beat Audio on Operative Pain and Anxiety in Cataract Surgery under Topical Anaesthesia: A Randomized Controlled Trial.

Loong LJ, Ling KK, Tai ELM, Kueh YC, Kuan G, Hussein A. · International journal of environmental research and public health · 2022 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A 61-person cataract-surgery trial reported lower operative pain, anxiety and heart rate with binaural-beat audio than with silent headphones.

Recorded limitations

A small procedure-specific study with a silence comparator. It does not separate beat effects from the effects of hearing calming audio.
