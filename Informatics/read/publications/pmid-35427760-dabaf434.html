Smartphone mindfulness meditation training reduces Pro-inflammatory gene expression in stressed adults: A randomized controlled trial

Dutcher JM, Cole SW, Williams AC, Creswell JD. · Brain, behavior, and immunity · 2022 · Randomized controlled trial; registered single-blind (outcome assessors)

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Mindfulness training reduced inferred NF-κB activity relative to the active comparison programme. Stress decreased in both groups, without a significant difference between them.

Recorded limitations

The molecular result concerns an inferred transcription-control pathway. It does not establish a measured change in every NF-κB-related gene or a clinical treatment effect.
