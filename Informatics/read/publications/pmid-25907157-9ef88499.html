Effectiveness and cost-effectiveness of mindfulness-based cognitive therapy compared with maintenance antidepressant treatment in the prevention of depressive relapse or recurrence (PREVENT): a randomised controlled trial

Kuyken W, Hayes R, Barrett B, Byng R, Dalgleish T, Kessler D, Lewis G, Watkins E, Brejcha C, Cardy J, Causley A, Cowderoy S, Evans A, Gradinger F, Kaur S, Lanham P, Morant N, Richards J, Shah P, Sutton H, Vicary R, Weaver A, Wilks J, Williams M, Taylor RS, Byford S. · Lancet (London, England) · 2015 · Randomized controlled trial; single-blind (research assessors)

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

PREVENT found no evidence that MBCT with taper support was superior to maintenance antidepressants for preventing relapse over two years (HR 0.89; 95% CI 0.67–1.18).

Recorded limitations

This was a superiority trial. Absence of a significant difference does not establish equivalence or noninferiority. Medication changes were supported within the trial.
