Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Attributed extracts from the research record

“the effect of oxytocin on spirituality was moderated by oxytocin-related genotypes.”

“These results provide the first experimental evidence that spirituality, endorsed by millions worldwide, appears to be supported by oxytocin.”
