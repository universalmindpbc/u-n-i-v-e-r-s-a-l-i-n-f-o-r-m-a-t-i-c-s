Effect of Mindfulness Meditation on Intraocular Pressure and Trabecular Meshwork Gene Expression: A Randomized Controlled Trial.

Dada T, Bhai N, Midha N, Shakrawal J, Kumar M, Chaurasia P, Gupta S, Angmo D, Yadav R, Dada R, Sihota R. · American journal of ophthalmology · 2021 · Single-blind randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 60 glaucoma patients, a three-week meditation adjunct reduced intraocular pressure relative to medication alone and was associated with trabecular-meshwork expression changes involving nitric-oxide and inflammatory pathways.

Recorded limitations

The measured tissue was ocular rather than brain tissue. The short, small trial and multiple molecular endpoints do not establish a general genomic mechanism or replacement for glaucoma care.
