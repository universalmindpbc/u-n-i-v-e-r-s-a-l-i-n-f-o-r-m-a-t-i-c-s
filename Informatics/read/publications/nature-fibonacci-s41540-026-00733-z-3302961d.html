Bacterial gene regulatory neural network as a biocomputing library of mathematical solvers

Adrian Ratwatte; Samitha Somathilaka; Ngoc Dan Thanh Cao; Xu Li; Sasitharan Balasubramaniam · npj Systems Biology and Applications · 2026 · Transcriptomic data analysis and gene-network computational modelling

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A search procedure identifies bacterial regulatory subnetworks whose expression responses match several finite mathematical tasks. One output-gene pattern approximates Fibonacci numbers across a small set of chemically encoded conditions.

Recorded limitations

Subnetworks are selected for agreement with the target pattern. The finite input set and computational stability analyses do not establish unrestricted arithmetic, natural selection for Fibonacci computation, or performance on unseen tasks.
