Dataset on gene expression in the elderly after Mindfulness Awareness Practice or Health Education Program.

Lim HW, Saw WY, Feng L, Lee YK, Mahendran R, Cheah IK, Rawtaer I, Kumar AP, Kua EH, Mahendran R, Tan EC. · Data in brief · 2018 · Gene-expression dataset from a randomized trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A blood gene-expression dataset from 60 older adults with mild cognitive impairment compares mindful-awareness practice with health education over nine months. It provides a reusable resource for analysing intervention-associated molecular patterns.

Recorded limitations

This is a data publication, not independent proof of a successful clinical or molecular effect. Participant overlap with associated trial reports must be retained in any synthesis.
