Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction Reduces Proinflammatory Gene Regulation But Not Systemic Inflammation Among Older Adults: A Randomized Controlled Trial

Lindsay EK, Marsland AL, Cole SW, Dutcher JM, Greco CM, Wright AGC, Brown KW, Creswell JD. · Psychosomatic medicine · 2024 · Randomized controlled trial; single-blind (laboratory assessors)

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

MBSR reduced inferred NF-κB activity relative to the active comparison programme (d=0.17; p=0.028). It did not reduce circulating IL-6 or C-reactive protein. CREB, IRF and glucocorticoid-receptor pathway differences were not statistically significant.

Recorded limitations

The gene-regulation signal was small and did not translate into a measured reduction in systemic inflammation. Pathway activity was inferred from RNA profiles.
