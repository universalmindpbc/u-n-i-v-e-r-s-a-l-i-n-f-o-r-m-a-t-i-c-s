Mindfulness meditation and improvement in sleep quality and daytime impairment among older adults with sleep disturbances: a randomized clinical trial.

Black DS, O'Reilly GA, Olmstead R, Breen EC, Irwin MR. · JAMA internal medicine · 2015 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 49 older adults with sleep disturbance, mindful-awareness training improved Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index scores by 1.8 points more than structured sleep-hygiene education, with a 95% confidence interval of 0.6 to 2.9.

Recorded limitations

The trial was small and measured immediate post-treatment effects. Inflammatory NF-kappaB measures did not differ between groups, so the sleep benefit does not establish that molecular pathway as its cause.
