Focusing performance of Fibonacci tiling-based zone plates

Adrián Garmendía-Martínez; Francisco M. Muñoz Pérez; Juan C. Castro Palacio; Walter D. Furlan; Vicente Ferrando; Juan A. Monsoriu · Scientific Reports · 2026 · Diffractive-optics simulations and experimental measurements

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Rectangular transparent and opaque elements generated from two-dimensional Fibonacci substitution rules form zone plates. Simulations and optical measurements characterize their axial focusing and transverse diffraction patterns.

Recorded limitations

The findings apply to the tested optical designs and measurements. They do not establish a general imaging advantage across biological samples or applications; appraisal here is abstract-level.
