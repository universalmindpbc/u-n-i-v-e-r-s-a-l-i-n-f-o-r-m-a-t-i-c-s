Quantum Walks in Periodic and Quasiperiodic Fibonacci Fibers

Dan T. Nguyen; Thien An Nguyen; Rostislav Khrapko; Daniel A. Nolan; Nicholas F. Borrelli · Scientific Reports · 2020 · Optical-fibre experiments and wave-propagation simulation

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Periodic and Fibonacci-arranged multicore ring fibres produce distinct propagation and localization patterns consistent with the numerical model. The authors present these fibres as a platform for quantum-walk analogues.

Recorded limitations

The measured classical coherent-wave behaviour models single-particle quantum walks. It does not by itself demonstrate entangled quantum computation or computational advantage.
