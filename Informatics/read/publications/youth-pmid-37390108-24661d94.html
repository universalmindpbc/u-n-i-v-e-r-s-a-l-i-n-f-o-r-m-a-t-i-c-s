Sixteen years of innovation in youth mental healthcare: Outcomes for young people attending Australia's headspace centre services.

Rickwood D, McEachran J, Saw A, Telford N, Trethowan J, McGorry P. · PloS one · 2023 · Observational study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Distress, functioning and quality-of-life scores improved over care; 70.96% of relevant outcome sample improved on at least one domain.

Recorded limitations

No comparator, variable treatments and missing follow-up; cannot attribute all improvement to service or apply percentage to every attendee.
