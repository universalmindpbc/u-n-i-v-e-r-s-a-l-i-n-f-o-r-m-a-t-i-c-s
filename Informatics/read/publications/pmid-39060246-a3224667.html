Distinct saliva DNA methylation profiles in relation to treatment outcome in youth with posttraumatic stress disorder.

Ensink JBM, Henneman P, Venema A, Zantvoord JB, den Kelder RO, Mannens MMAM, Lindauer RJL. · Translational Psychiatry · 2024 · Longitudinal DNA-methylation study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The study included 46 young people with PTSD and 41 trauma-exposed controls, aged 8–18. Saliva DNA-methylation differences associated with treatment response included C18orf63 and the stress-related CRHR2 locus.

Recorded limitations

Treatment combined evidence from EMDR and trauma-focused CBT. Saliva findings do not establish a brain-tissue effect or isolate eye movements.
