Self-administered EMDR therapy: potential solution for expanding the availability of psychotherapy for PTSD or unregulated recipe for disaster?

Lauren Z. Waterman; Maxwell Cooper · BJPsych Open · 2020 · Review

Original publication

Attributed extracts from the research record

“The results indicated significantly reduced symptoms of PTSD, depression, anxiety, distress and disability between pre-treatment and 3 month follow-up.”

Recorded limitations

The quoted improvement describes an earlier feasibility study combining internet CBT and EMDR with clinician contact. It is not a result from a new randomized trial of unguided self-help.
