The effect of online mindfulness training on connectedness to oneself, to others and to nature in students.

Jansen P, Hoja S, Cassar L, Neu TM. · Applied psychology. Health and well-being · 2026 · Randomized controlled trial report · double blinding not verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Mindfulness, self-compassion and nature connection improved; perceived stress, flourishing and prosociality did not differ between groups.

Recorded limitations

Inactive control, adherence-selected main analysis and predominantly women. Within-person prediction of stress is not a randomized stress effect.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
