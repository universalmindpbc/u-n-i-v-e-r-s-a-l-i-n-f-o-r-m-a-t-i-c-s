Effects of spiritual mantram repetition on HIV outcomes: a randomized controlled trial.

Bormann JE, Gifford AL, Shively M, Smith TL, Redwine L, Kelly A, Becker S, Gershwin M, Bone P, Belding W. · Journal of behavioral medicine · 2006 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Among 93 adults living with HIV, mantram repetition improved trait anger and selected spiritual-wellbeing measures relative to an attention-control programme.

Recorded limitations

Benefits were outcome-specific. Associations between practice frequency and improvement are observational within the trial and do not establish a dose-response effect or an HIV disease-modifying benefit.
