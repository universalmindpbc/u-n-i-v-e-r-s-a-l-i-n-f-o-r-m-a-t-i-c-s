Universality and quantum criticality in quasiperiodic spin chains

Utkarsh Agrawal; Sarang Gopalakrishnan; Romain Vasseur · Nature Communications · 2020 · Renormalization-group theory and numerical modelling

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Real-space renormalization maps broad classes of quasiperiodic spin-chain modulations to discrete self-similar sequences. The Heisenberg-chain fixed-point sequence follows Fibonacci deflation rules.

Recorded limitations

The conclusions depend on the studied one-dimensional models and renormalization assumptions. They do not constitute experimental evidence for an arbitrary material or biological system.
