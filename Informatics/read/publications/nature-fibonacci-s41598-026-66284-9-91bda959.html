The new exponential Fibonacci neighborhood as QUBO generated on a D-Wave quantum computing environment for the permutational scheduling problem

Wojciech Bożejko; Mariusz Uchroński; Mieczysław Wodecki · Scientific Reports · 2026 · Combinatorial construction and quantum-annealing computational experiment

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The authors construct a permutation neighbourhood with Fibonacci-number cardinality and encode it using a binary sequence of length n−1. They test a related QUBO formulation on a D-Wave system for a single-machine scheduling problem.

Recorded limitations

A compact encoding and tested scheduling results do not prove a general quantum speedup. Performance depends on the neighbourhood restriction, instance selection and hardware; appraisal here is abstract-level.
