A randomised active-controlled trial to examine the effects of an online mindfulness intervention on executive control, critical thinking and key thinking dispositions in a university student sample.

Noone C, Hogan MJ. · BMC psychology · 2018 · Randomized controlled trial report · verified double blind

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

No between-group benefit on primary executive control, critical thinking or thinking dispositions, or secondary wellbeing/affect measures.

Recorded limitations

Small predominantly female university sample; attrition and limited training period. Commercial Headspace app is distinct from Australian headspace services.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
