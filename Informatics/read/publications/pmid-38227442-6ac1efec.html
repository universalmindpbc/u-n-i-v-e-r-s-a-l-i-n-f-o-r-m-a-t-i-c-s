Eye movement desensitization and reprocessing (EMDR) therapy compared to usual treatment for posttraumatic stress disorder in adults with psychosis in forensic settings: Randomized controlled trial.

Every-Palmer S, Flewett T, Dean S, Hansby O, Freeland A, Weatherall M, Bell E. · Psychological trauma : theory, research, practice and policy · 2024 · Single-blind randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 24 forensic patients with psychosis and PTSD, blinded assessments favoured EMDR over routine-care waitlist for clinician-rated PTSD symptoms. The estimated difference averaged across follow-ups was 11.4 points, with a 95% confidence interval of 1.3 to 21.4.

Recorded limitations

Recruitment was curtailed by pandemic restrictions, leaving a small sample. Psychotic symptoms and adverse events did not differ significantly, so effectiveness claims remain specific to the measured PTSD outcomes.
