Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Depression and anxiety improved at one month, effects around d 0.5. Elevated-symptom anxiety subgroup CI crossed zero.

Recorded limitations

Two schools, shorter-than-planned recruitment and changed schedule, short follow-up. Registration November 2021 follows August recruitment despite preregistration language.
