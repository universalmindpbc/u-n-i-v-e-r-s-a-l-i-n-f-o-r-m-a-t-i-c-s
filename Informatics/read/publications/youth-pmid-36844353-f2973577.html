Effects of online mindfulness-based interventions on the mental health of university students: A systematic review and meta-analysis.

Gong XG, Wang LP, Rong G, Zhang DN, Zhang AY, Liu C. · Frontiers in psychology · 2023 · Systematic review / meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Pooled estimates favoured online mindfulness for depression, anxiety and stress; wellbeing interval touched zero.

Recorded limitations

Heterogeneous interventions, mostly uncertain masking, English-only published evidence and few trials. Review-level masking classifications are not inherited by individual trials; overlaps other reviews and included primary studies.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
