Brief psychological intervention with traumatized young women: the efficacy of eye movement desensitization and reprocessing.

Scheck MM, Schaeffer JA, Gillette C. · Journal of traumatic stress · 1998 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Sixty women aged 16–25 received two sessions of EMDR or active listening. Both groups improved, with larger immediate changes in the EMDR group.

Recorded limitations

A small, early trial with a mixed adolescent/young-adult sample. Immediate outcomes do not establish durable effects or an exclusively 18–24 result.
