Examining the effects of binaural beat music on sleep quality, heart rate variability, and depression in older people with poor sleep quality in a long-term care institution: A randomized controlled trial.

Lin PH, Fu SH, Lee YC, Yang SY, Li YL. · Geriatrics & gerontology international · 2024 · Single-blind randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Among 64 older adults with poor sleep in long-term care, binaural beats embedded in familiar music improved sleep measures and selected autonomic measures relative to the same music without beats over 14 days.

Recorded limitations

The short intervention and single institutional setting limit inference about durable sleep improvement. Physiological changes do not establish a molecular mechanism.
