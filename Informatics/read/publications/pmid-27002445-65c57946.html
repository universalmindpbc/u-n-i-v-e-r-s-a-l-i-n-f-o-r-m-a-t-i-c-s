Effect of Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction vs Cognitive Behavioral Therapy or Usual Care on Back Pain and Functional Limitations in Adults With Chronic Low Back Pain: A Randomized Clinical Trial

Cherkin DC, Sherman KJ, Balderson BH, Cook AJ, Anderson ML, Hawkes RJ, Hansen KE, Turner JA. · JAMA · 2016 · Randomized controlled trial; single-blind (interviewers)

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

At 26 weeks, meaningful functional improvement occurred in 60.5% receiving MBSR, 57.7% receiving CBT and 44.1% receiving usual care. Both active treatments improved pain outcomes; no significant difference separated MBSR and CBT.

Recorded limitations

These are clinical pain and function outcomes, not molecular measurements. The trial does not establish superiority over CBT.
