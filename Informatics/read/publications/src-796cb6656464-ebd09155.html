Use of EMDR-Derived Self-Help Intervention in Children in the Period of COVID-19: A Randomized-Controlled Study

Mehmet Karadag; Zehra Topal; Ravza Nur Ezer; Cem Gokcen · Journal of EMDR Practice and Research · 2021 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication

Attributed extracts from the research record

“The intervention group had significant pre–post improvement on all but one subscale, while the waitlist group showed significant increase in state anxiety on the STAIC. In conclusion, posttraumatic stress was found to be high in children during the COVID-19 outbreak period, and EMDR-derived self-help intervention appeared to be an effective psychosocial intervention tool.”

Recorded limitations

Parent-supported story-based intervention during COVID-19. This is not a test of unsupervised trauma processing or a complete clinical EMDR protocol.
