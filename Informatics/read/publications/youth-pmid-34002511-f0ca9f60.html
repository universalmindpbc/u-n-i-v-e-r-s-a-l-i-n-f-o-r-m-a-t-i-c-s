The Horyzons project: a randomized controlled trial of a novel online social therapy to maintain treatment effects from specialist first-episode psychosis services.

Alvarez-Jimenez M, Koval P, Schmaal L, Bendall S, O'Sullivan S, Cagliarini D, D'Alfonso S, Rice S, Valentine L, Penn DL, Miles C, Russon P, Phillips J, McEnery C, Lederman R, Killackey E, Mihalopoulos C, Gonzalez-Blanch C, Gilbertson T, Lal S, Cotton SM, Herrman H, McGorry PD, Gleeson JFM. · World psychiatry : official journal of the World Psychiatric Association (WPA) · 2021 · Randomized controlled trial report · single masked role verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Primary social functioning null: difference −0.29 (95% CI −4.20 to 3.63). Employment/education secondary odds favoured Horyzons (OR 5.55, wide CI 1.09–28.23); emergency visits lower; hospitalisation difference not significant.

Recorded limitations

Primary social-function outcome null; secondary vocational result imprecise. Dose associations are not randomised effects; specialist remission sample may have ceiling effects.
