Multisensory gamma stimulation promotes glymphatic clearance of amyloid

Murdock MH, Yang CY, Sun N, Pao PC, Blanco-Duque C, Kahn MC, Kim T, Lavoie NS, Victor MB, Islam MR, Galiana F, Leary N, Wang S, Bubnys A, Ma E, Akay LA, Sneve M, Qian Y, Lai C, McCarthy MM, Kopell N, Kellis M, Piatkevich KD, Boyden ES, Tsai LH. · Nature · 2024 · Mouse Alzheimer-disease model experiments

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Combined 40 Hz sensory stimulation increased brain-fluid clearance and reduced amyloid in the tested mouse model. Mechanistic experiments implicated VIP interneurons and glymphatic function.

Recorded limitations

This is multisensory animal research. It does not isolate visual effects, establish clinical efficacy or test OXTR–Fibonacci relationships.
