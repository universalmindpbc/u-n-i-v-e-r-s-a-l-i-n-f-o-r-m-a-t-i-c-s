Quasi-periodic distributed feedback laser

Lukas Mahler; Alessandro Tredicucci; Fabio Beltram; Christoph Walther; Jérôme Faist; Harvey E. Beere; David A. Ritchie; Diederik S. Wiersma · Nature Photonics · 2010 · Laser-device experiment using an aperiodic grating

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

An electrically pumped terahertz quantum-cascade laser uses a Fibonacci distributed-feedback grating. Its engineered spectrum supports multicolour operation and directional output behaviour beyond the periodic designs used for comparison.

Recorded limitations

The results concern a fabricated optical resonator. They do not establish general computational acceleration or a biological mechanism; appraisal here is abstract-level.
