Effects of the Transcendental Meditation Program on Substance Use among University Students.

Haaga DA, Grosswald S, Gaylord-King C, Rainforth M, Tanner M, Travis F, Nidich S, Schneider RH. · Cardiology research and practice · 2011 · Randomized controlled trial report · single masked role verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Smoking and illicit drug use did not differ. An alcohol-use interaction favoured men receiving TM, not women.

Recorded limitations

Self-report, waitlist, 25% attrition and sex subgroup finding; not a general substance-use benefit. Same Washington university recruitment, dates and criteria as the BP report; treat as overlapping cohort.
