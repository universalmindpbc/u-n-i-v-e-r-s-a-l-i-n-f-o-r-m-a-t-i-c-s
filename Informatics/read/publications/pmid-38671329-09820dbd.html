Serotonin Transporter (SLC6A4) and FK506-Binding Protein 5 (FKBP5) Genotype and Methylation Relationships with Response to Meditation in Veterans with PTSD

Lee A, Thuras P, Baller J, Jiao C, Guo B, Erbes CR, Polusny MA, Liu C, Wu B, Lim KO, Bishop JR. · Molecular neurobiology · 2024 · Secondary analysis of two intervention studies

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

SLC6A4 genotype was associated with symptom improvement among veterans exposed to early-life trauma. Change in a group of nine FKBP5 methylation sites was associated with greater improvement (OR 2.8; 95% CI 1.1–7.1).

Recorded limitations

Genotype and DNA methylation were measured; this is not direct RNA-expression evidence. Associations across two meditation studies do not isolate a treatment mechanism.
