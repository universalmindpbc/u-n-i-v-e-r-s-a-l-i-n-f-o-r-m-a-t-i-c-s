Long-term effectiveness of eye movement desensitization and reprocessing in children and adolescents with medically related subthreshold post-traumatic stress disorder: a randomized controlled trial.

Meentken MG, van der Mheen M, van Beynum IM, Aendekerk EWC, Legerstee JS, van der Ende J, Del Canho R, Lindauer RJL, Hillegers MHJ, Helbing WA, Moll HA, Utens EMWJ. · European journal of cardiovascular nursing · 2021 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Among 74 previously hospitalized children aged 4–15 with subthreshold PTSD, EMDR did not outperform care as usual on the primary PTSD outcome through eight months. Parent-reported blood-injection-injury phobia improved more with EMDR.

Recorded limitations

Subthreshold medically related PTSD differs from diagnosed PTSD after other trauma. Keep this neutral primary result alongside positive trials.
