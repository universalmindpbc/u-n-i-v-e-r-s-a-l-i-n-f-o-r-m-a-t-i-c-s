Epigenetic markers of disease risk and psychotherapy response in anxiety disorders - a longitudinal analysis of the DNA methylome

Domschke K, Schiele MA, Crespo Salvador Ó, Zillich L, Lipovsek J, Pittig A, Heinig I, Ridderbusch IC, Straube B, Richter J, Hollandt M, Plag J, Fydrich T, Koelkebeck K, Weber H, Lueken U, Dannlowski U, Margraf J, Schneider S, Binder EB, Ströhle A, Rief W, Kircher T, Pauli P, Hamm A, Arolt V, Hoyer J, Wittchen HU, Erhardt-Lehmann A, Köttgen A, Schlosser P, Deckert J. · Molecular Psychiatry · 2025 · Longitudinal epigenome-wide association study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The study identified disease-associated methylation sites and suggestive baseline predictors and longitudinal changes related to psychotherapy response.

Recorded limitations

Treatment-response signals were suggestive rather than definitive epigenome-wide findings. Association does not establish that verbal communication caused the molecular changes.
