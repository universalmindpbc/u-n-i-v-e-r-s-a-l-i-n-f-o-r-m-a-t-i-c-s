Maternal DRD2, SLC6A3, and OXTR genotypes as potential moderators of the relation between maternal history of care and maternal cortisol secretion in the context of mother-infant separation

Ludmer JA, Jamieson B, Gonzalez A, Levitan R, Kennedy J, Villani V, Masellis M, Basile VS, Atkinson L. · Biological psychology · 2017

Original publication · Original publication

Attributed extracts from the research record

“Multilevel models revealed that history of care had a greater influence on maternal baseline cortisol (but not cortisol trajectory) for mothers with more plasticity alleles of SLC6A3 (10R) and OXTR (G), relative to mothers with fewer or no plasticity alleles.”
“OXTR moderates between maternal history of care and cortisol during infant stress.”
“SLC6A3 moderates between maternal history of care and cortisol during infant stress.”
