Shared Transcriptomic and Epigenomic Signatures Between Traumatic Brain Injury and Music Exposure: An Integrative Proof of Concept

Gómez-Carballa A, Pischedda S, Mallah NEZ, Nowak W, Martinón-Torres F, Navarro L, Salas A. · ASN neuro · 2026 · Integrative secondary-data analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The analysis identified overlapping molecular signatures between music-exposure datasets and traumatic-brain-injury datasets, including 154 shared differentially expressed genes.

Recorded limitations

Music was not administered to the brain-injury cohorts in this comparison. Shared signatures do not demonstrate a treatment effect.
