The effect of eye movement desensitization and reprocessing-based online group counseling for university students: Psychological need satisfaction, psychological resilience, psychological well-being, and small 't' trauma.

Yilmaz Dinç S, Sapmaz F. · Psychological trauma : theory, research, practice and policy · 2023 · Controlled experimental study

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

EMDR-based online group counselling for university students reported improvements in psychological need satisfaction, resilience, wellbeing and several small-t trauma measures across repeated assessments.

Recorded limitations

University enrolment does not establish an exclusively 18–24 sample. The abstract does not provide sufficient age or masking detail; body-sense effects were not significant.
