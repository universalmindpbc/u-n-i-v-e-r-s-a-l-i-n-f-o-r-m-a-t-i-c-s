High-Color-Temperature Lighting Is Associated With Activation of MAPK/ERK-nNOS Signaling and MMP-2-Related Pathways in Ocular Tissues

Chiang YW, Chang JY, Yeh KL, Lin RY, Yeh SM. · Investigative ophthalmology & visual science · 2026 · Controlled mouse lighting experiment

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

After 21 days, higher-colour-temperature lighting was associated with increased nNOS, IL-6, TNF and other signalling proteins, without detectable axial-length or structural changes.

Recorded limitations

Protein abundance is not an RNA-expression measurement. The result concerns a defined mouse exposure and includes inflammatory responses rather than an established benefit.
