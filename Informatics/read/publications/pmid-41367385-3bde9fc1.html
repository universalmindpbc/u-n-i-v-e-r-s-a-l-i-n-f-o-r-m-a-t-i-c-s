Decoding the peripheral transcriptomic and meta-genomic response to music in autism spectrum disorder via saliva-based RNA sequencing

Cavenaghi A, Mallah NEZ, Navarro L, Martinón-Torres F, Gómez-Carballa A, Salas A. · Frontiers in Molecular Biosciences · 2025 · Exploratory human pilot

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The pilot detected exploratory RNA and microbial changes after music exposure, including signals involving HERC6, TSPAN5 and REM2.

Recorded limitations

Five participants and exploratory integration of sequencing methods limit inference. This is not evidence of a clinical treatment effect.
