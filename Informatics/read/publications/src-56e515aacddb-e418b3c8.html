A randomized controlled trial on effects of the Transcendental Meditation program on blood pressure, psychological distress, and coping in young adults.

Nidich SI, Rainforth MV, Haaga DA, Hagelin J, Salerno JW, Travis F, Tanner M, Gaylord-King C, Grosswald S, Schneider RH. · American Journal of Hypertension · 2009 · Randomized controlled trial report · single masked role verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Overall blood-pressure differences were not statistically significant. The hypertension-risk subgroup had larger reductions, alongside reported improvements in distress and coping. Subgroup cardiovascular findings do not demonstrate a general youth mental-health effect.

Recorded limitations

About 30% missing posttests, inactive control, three-month duration and mixed-age student sample. Overall BP differences were nonsignificant; subgroup BP finding is not universal hypertension prevention.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
