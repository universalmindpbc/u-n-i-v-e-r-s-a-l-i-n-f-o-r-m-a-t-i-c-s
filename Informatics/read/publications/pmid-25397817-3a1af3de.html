Mindful attention increases and mediates psychological outcomes following mantram repetition practice in veterans with posttraumatic stress disorder.

Bormann JE, Oman D, Walter KH, Johnson BD. · Medical care · 2014 · Secondary analysis of a randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In a 146-veteran trial, adding mantram repetition to usual care increased mindful attention; changes in mindful attention statistically mediated improvements in PTSD symptoms, depression and wellbeing.

Recorded limitations

This is a secondary mechanistic analysis of the underlying trial. Statistical mediation does not prove the causal mechanism, and the comparison did not isolate attention or expectancy effects.
