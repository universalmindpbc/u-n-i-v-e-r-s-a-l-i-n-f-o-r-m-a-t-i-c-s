Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction training reduces loneliness and pro-inflammatory gene expression in older adults: a small randomized controlled trial

Creswell JD, Irwin MR, Burklund LJ, Lieberman MD, Arevalo JM, Ma J, Breen EC, Cole SW. · Brain, behavior, and immunity · 2012 · Randomized pilot trial; single-blind (study assessors)

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

MBSR reduced loneliness and the NF-κB-associated inflammatory gene-expression profile relative to waitlist. The C-reactive protein difference did not reach statistical significance (p=0.075).

Attributed extracts from the research record

“This work provides an initial indication that mindfulness based stress reduction may be a novel treatment approach for reducing loneliness and related pro-inflammatory gene expression in older adults”

Recorded limitations

A small pilot without an active comparator. The molecular finding is a pathway-level expression profile.
