Trauma-focused therapy in early psychosis: results of a feasibility randomized controlled trial of EMDR for psychosis (EMDRp) in early intervention settings.

Varese F, Sellwood W, Pulford D, Awenat Y, Bird L, Bhutani G, Carter LA, Davies L, Aseem S, Davis C, Hefferman-Clarke R, Hilton C, Horne G, Keane D, Logie R, Malkin D, Potter F, van den Berg D, Zia S, Bentall RP. · Psychological medicine · 2024 · Single-blind randomized feasibility trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 60 early-psychosis service users, a 16-session EMDR adaptation met trial feasibility targets and showed promising clinical signals at six months. PTSD symptoms and general-health signals remained evident at 12 months.

Recorded limitations

The study was designed to establish feasibility. Follow-up completion was 70% at 12 months, and the efficacy signals require confirmation in a larger trial.
