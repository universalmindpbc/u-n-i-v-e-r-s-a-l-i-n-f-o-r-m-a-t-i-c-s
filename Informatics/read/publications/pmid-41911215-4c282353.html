Preoperative binaural beats reduce remimazolam dosage and enhance safety in anesthesia induction: A randomized controlled trial.

Kim HC, Sohn JY, Kim MH, Kim YJ, Chang CH, Seo JH. · PloS one · 2026 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 72 surgical patients, 30 minutes of 1 Hz binaural beats reduced the mean remimazolam dose required for loss of consciousness from 17.7 to 15.0 mg; hypotension occurred in 6% versus 28% of participants.

Recorded limitations

A single-centre anaesthesia study with no auditory intervention in the comparison arm. EEG differences were not significant, so the proposed entrainment mechanism remains unconfirmed.
