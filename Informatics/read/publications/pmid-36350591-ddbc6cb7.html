Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction vs Escitalopram for the Treatment of Adults With Anxiety Disorders: A Randomized Clinical Trial

Hoge EA, Bui E, Mete M, Dutton MA, Baker AW, Simon NM. · JAMA psychiatry · 2023 · Randomized controlled trial; single-blind (clinical assessors)

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

MBSR met the prespecified noninferiority criterion against escitalopram for anxiety severity at eight weeks. Mean CGI-S improvement was 1.35 points with MBSR and 1.43 with escitalopram.

Recorded limitations

This result applies to a structured, teacher-led MBSR course and the trial’s noninferiority margin. It is not a test of an app or gene expression.
