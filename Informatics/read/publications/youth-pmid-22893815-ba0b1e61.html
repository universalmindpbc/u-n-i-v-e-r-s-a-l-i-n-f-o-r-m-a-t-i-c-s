A randomised comparison of cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) and eye movement desensitisation and reprocessing (EMDR) in disaster-exposed children.

de Roos C, Greenwald R, den Hollander-Gijsman M, Noorthoorn E, van Buuren S, de Jongh A. · European journal of psychotraumatology · 2011 · Randomized controlled trial report · single masked role verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Symptoms improved in both active treatment groups and gains persisted at follow-up. EMDR reached treatment goals in fewer sessions. With no inactive comparison, improvement alone cannot establish the treatment-specific effect.

Recorded limitations

Small broad age range, one disaster context and no untreated arm; randomization was by coin flip.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
