Online, low-volume meditation does not alter immune-related biomarkers

Graham B, Jin Y, Bazeley P, Husni E, Calabrese LH. · Brain, behavior, & immunity - health · 2022 · Randomized pilot trial; single-blind

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The trial found no significant between-group differences in the prespecified immune-related gene set, wider RNA expression or other tested biomarkers. Stress improved earlier in the music group and later in the mindfulness group.

Recorded limitations

A small trial of brief online practice in otherwise healthy nurses. The result concerns this dose and delivery; it does not establish the absence of effects from other programmes.
