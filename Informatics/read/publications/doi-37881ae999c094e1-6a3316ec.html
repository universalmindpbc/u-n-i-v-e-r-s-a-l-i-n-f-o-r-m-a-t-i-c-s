The effectiveness of trauma-based therapies for Aboriginal and non-Aboriginal children and adolescents in out-of-home care

Debbie Haynes; Sean A. Halpin; Laurel Williams; Bronwyn Chambers; Frances Heritage Martin · First Nations Health and Wellbeing - The Lowitja Journal · 2025 · Peer-reviewed research

Original publication

Recorded summary

Analysis of 144 children and adolescents in the LINKS programme reports reduced difficulties and trauma symptoms, with positive findings for EMDR and parent–child interaction therapy.

Recorded limitations

No randomised treatment assignment or untreated control; the programme includes multiple therapies and cultural adaptations.
