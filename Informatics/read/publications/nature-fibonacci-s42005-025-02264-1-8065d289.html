Fibonacci-modulation-induced multiple topological Anderson insulators

Ruijiang Ji; Zhihao Xu · Communications Physics · 2025 · Theoretical and numerical topological-chain model

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A one-dimensional spin-orbit-coupled chain with Fibonacci modulation exhibits multiple modelled topological Anderson-insulator phases and multifractal wave functions as parameters vary.

Recorded limitations

The paper proposes a cold-atom realization; the modelled phases are not reported as an experimental observation. The result remains specific to the chosen Hamiltonian and modulation.
