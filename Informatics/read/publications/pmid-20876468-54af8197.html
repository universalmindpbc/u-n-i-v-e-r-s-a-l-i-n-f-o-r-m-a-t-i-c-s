MS quality of life, depression, and fatigue improve after mindfulness training: a randomized trial.

Grossman P, Kappos L, Gensicke H, D'Souza M, Mohr DC, Penner IK, Steiner C. · Neurology · 2010 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 150 people with multiple sclerosis, eight weeks of mindfulness training improved nonphysical quality of life, depression and fatigue relative to usual care, with benefits persisting at six months.

Recorded limitations

The comparison did not control for instructor attention, and outcomes concerned wellbeing rather than disease progression. The paper classifies its evidence as Class III.
