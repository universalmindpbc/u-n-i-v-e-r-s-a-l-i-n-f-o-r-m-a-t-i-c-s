Evaluating the Efficacy of a 5-Min Music Listening Intervention for State Anxiety Reduction in College Students: A Randomized Controlled Trial.

Soqia J, Yakoub-Agha L, Alameer MB, Nahas L, Mohamad L, Antoun I, Almohsen C, Mohsen S. · Health science reports · 2025 · Randomized controlled trial report · double blinding not verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

No significant anxiety reduction between arms (p=0.622); neither arm's within-group change significant.

Recorded limitations

Small single-faculty study, immediate outcomes only, baseline anxiety differences; no sound-sensitivity measurement.
