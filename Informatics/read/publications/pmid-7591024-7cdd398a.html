A randomised controlled trial of stress reduction for hypertension in older African Americans.

Schneider RH, Staggers F, Alxander CN, Sheppard W, Rainforth M, Kondwani K, Smith S, King CG. · Hypertension (Dallas, Tex. : 1979) · 1995 · Single-blind randomized controlled trial (outcome assessors blinded)

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A trial enrolling 127 older Black adults with hypertension found greater three-month blood-pressure reductions with Transcendental Meditation than progressive muscle relaxation or health education, using blinded blood-pressure observers.

Recorded limitations

Sixteen participants were lost to follow-up. The older, short-duration trial does not establish sustained cardiovascular-event reduction or replacement of antihypertensive care.
