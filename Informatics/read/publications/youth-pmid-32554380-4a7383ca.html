Effectiveness of an 8-Week Web-Based Mindfulness Virtual Community Intervention for University Students on Symptoms of Stress, Anxiety, and Depression: Randomized Controlled Trial.

El Morr C, Ritvo P, Ahmad F, Moineddin R, MVC Team. · JMIR mental health · 2020 · Randomized controlled trial report · double blinding not verified

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Depression (β −2.21) and anxiety (β −4.82) improved; perceived stress did not (p=.48).

Recorded limitations

Mostly female, self-report, inactive control and assumed digital access; multiple components prevent isolating mindfulness.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
