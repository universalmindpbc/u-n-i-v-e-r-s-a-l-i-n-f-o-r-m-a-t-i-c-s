Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Small overall benefits; active-control advantages limited to mindfulness, depression and anxiety/stress.

Recorded limitations

Earlier evidence base superseded/extended by 2022 update; small studies and heterogeneous controls. Do not add overlapping review participant counts.
