The effects of neuromodulation through brainwave entrainment on pain in women with fibromyalgia: a randomized controlled clinical trial with follow-up.

de Souza G, Daux Buffon L, Hardt da Silva R, Galassi TO, Dutra AR, Medeiros N, de Paiva BC, de Brito VJ, Nienkotter L, Baldança HDS, Goedert SH, Rezin GT, da Silva MR, de Oliveira MP, Petronilho F, Mathias K, Tiscoski ADB, Mazzardo-Martins L, Traebert ESA, Piovezan AP, Traebert JL, Caumo W, Schleip R, Bobinski F, Cidral-Filho FJ, Martins DF. · Pain medicine (Malden, Mass.) · 2026 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

In 84 women with fibromyalgia, eight weeks of headset-based brainwave entrainment produced improvements in pain and anxiety, with changes in quality of life, oxidative stress and circulating BDNF also examined.

Recorded limitations

Music therapy improved several outcomes as well. The abstract does not provide a consistent set of between-group effect estimates; the mixed headset intervention and protein measurements do not establish a gene-expression mechanism.
