Initial focus · Computer vision for genomics

Inspired by research into how geometric architectures and Fibonacci-ordered dynamics can protect quantum information, we are investigating whether geometry derived from Fibonacci Pisano periods can improve the computational efficiency of hyperspectral image analysis for genomics.

A measurable first step

Our proposed approach explores equilateral triangular meshes and tetrahedral models, comparing computational cost, processing speed and accuracy against established methods.

The initial biological application is to investigate spectral patterns potentially associated with OXTR gene expression, evaluated against independent molecular measurements.
