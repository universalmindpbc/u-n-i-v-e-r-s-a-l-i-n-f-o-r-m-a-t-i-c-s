Our hypothesis is that the Alpha, Beta, Gamma and Epsilon sequences within Fibonacci M9 P24 may offer useful descriptions of biological timing, cellular division and molecular organisation in the context of OXTR.

Our initial research is specific to OXTR, the oxytocin receptor. We plan to investigate these proposed relationships through bioinformatics, primarily in silico modelling. Our starting points are the research targets introduced on the homepage:

  • Circadian rhythms
  • Valence electron orbitals
  • Molecular docking
  • π stacking
  • Mitosis

These infographics introduce four themes informing this initial OXTR investigation. The wider literature provides context for the mechanisms and methods.
