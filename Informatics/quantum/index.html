    Fibonacci pulses and longer-lived quantum information

    DUMITRESCU ET AL. · NATURE 607, 463–467 · 2022

    Laser pulses arranged in a Fibonacci pattern kept quantum information stable for longer in the atoms of a quantum computer.

    ~1.5 secondsRepeating pulse pattern
    ~5.5 secondsFibonacci-ordered pattern

    These observations concerned the edge qubits of a ten-ion processor. The Fibonacci-driven information remained coherent for the full 5.5-second observation period. The 1.5-second comparison is specific to that experiment, not a universal qubit lifetime.

    The authors attribute the protection to symmetries emerging from the pulse ordering. Their experiment did not test a hexagonal atom arrangement or M9 P24.

    A stimulus for our investigation

    Quantum Atlas investigates the hexagonal and triangular structures in Fibonacci’s modular representations across M1–M1001. We are exploring whether those relationships have measurable consequences in quantum systems, using Fibonacci, Phi Ratio, Alpha, Beta, Gamma and Epsilon. OXTR DNA supplies the first biological test inputs.

    The geometric mechanism illustrated below is our working hypothesis. Odom’s construction and the honeycomb theorem establish mathematical relationships; a connection to quantum coherence remains to be tested.
