Model & numerical checks

H / ℏ = Ω(t)/2 · (X₀ + X₁) − Δ(t) · (n₀ + n₁) + V · n₀n₁

V = C₆/r⁶, C₆ = 5.42 × 10⁻²⁴ rad m⁶/s. Initial state |gg⟩. Phase is zero. The drive ramps over the first and final 2.5% of the pulse; detuning sweeps linearly between them.

The browser solves the four-state Schrödinger equation with RK4, repeats at half the step size, and requires probability difference and norm error below 10⁻⁶. This check measures numerical convergence, not physical fidelity.

Ideal closed system. No noise, atom loss or preparation/readout errors. g = ground; r = Rydberg excitation. Basis order: gg, gr, rg, rr, with atom 0 first.
