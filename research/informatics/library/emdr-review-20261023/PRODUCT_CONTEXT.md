# Universal Mind I AM: product context

Ben clarified this scope on 23 October 2026 while the Vision edition was being prepared.

The general product is a free, optional entertainment experience investigating short-term stress reduction and increased felt connection. It combines saccadic eye movements, brain entrainment frequencies, silent mantra and gentle, low-grade, non-acute themes conveyed through popular song lyrics. A research chatbot may invite gentle reflection before a session. Pre/post ratings belong to the research protocol; ordinary entertainment use does not require them. Referral pathways complement existing care. This service is not intended to replace clinicians or medication.

Audio and video rooms already support clinician-guided groups of up to nine people. This room capability is distinct from the specialist clinical programme and its assessment, clinical governance and care arrangements. Ben envisages that programme as a separate, niche service for people with acute trauma. Do not describe the existing rooms as a merely planned technical feature. Do not equate optional entertainment with clinical EMDR or claim that clinical PTSD evidence validates the combined entertainment experience.

The immediate research outcomes are short-term stress and felt connection. PTSD remission, gene expression and other molecular outcomes require their own measurements and study designs. Component evidence establishes research context; the combined intervention requires direct evaluation. Headspace and UNICEF are potential future organisations of interest, not established partners.

The supplementary discussion of bilateral stimulation, Flash and self-administered protocols is a research comparison, not approval to add all those interventions to the website or launch a new treatment. Preserve the already approved original self-help literature in the Vision edition.
