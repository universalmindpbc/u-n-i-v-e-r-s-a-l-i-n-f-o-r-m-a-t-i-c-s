# Epsilon eligibility
Epsilon requires a sequential mapping containing all three nodal values 3, 6 and 9. This is the project definition, not a claim that other geometric or Eulerian paths cannot exist.
M1 through M8: Epsilon is null because 9 is unavailable under native-modulus labels (zero may be displayed as the modulus).
For M9 and above, eligibility is necessary but not sufficient: verify values and traversal before assigning Epsilon.
Do not draw an Epsilon path in a combined view when Epsilon is null.
