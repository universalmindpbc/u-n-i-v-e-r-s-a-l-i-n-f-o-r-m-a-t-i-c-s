## Proposed call-out: Initial observations and open directions

This first experiment makes published OXTR DNA elements, experimental regions and predicted matches available for comparison with Alpha, Beta, Gamma and Epsilon. We have not yet established that these geometries predict binding-site locations. The current views highlight the importance of separating evidence sources and testing the effects of coordinate mapping and compression.

The investigation remains open. Alongside binding-site analysis, we are considering molecular organisation, circadian timing and cellular division in OXTR research. Each direction needs its own measurements and a defined test of the proposed relationship.

## Basis for the discussion

- The software now provides traceable coordinate comparisons, source separation, positive binding outcomes, original data and reproducible mappings. These are useful research capabilities, not an association result.
- Two recovered study elements are an incomplete biological sample. ENCODE pools distinct experiments, factors and cell contexts; JASPAR pools motif models and predictions. Those collections are not three interchangeable maps of all active sites in one cell.
- Populating every M9 node neither establishes nor refutes predictive geometry. Compression merges distinct positions. Counts and ordered records remain available, but predictive value requires a specified statistic and appropriate controls.
- Next for binding sites: agree the biological context and prediction rule before looking for the strongest fit; test against matched controls and unseen observations. Record sensitivity to the DNA origin, interval-centre choice and window size. No such statistical test has run in this revision.
- Molecular structure is a separate hypothesis with directly measurable distances, angles and aromatic-ring orientations. Compare structural coordinates, including tyrosine interactions, rather than infer a match from both drawings containing six-sided shapes. Do not assume a specific pi-stacking contact without checking the receptor structure.
- Circadian timing requires measurements over time. A 24-position mathematical period has no intrinsic unit of hours. Test a proposed time mapping against a conventional periodic model.
- Cellular division requires measured division or proliferation dynamics in a defined system. The arithmetic doubling sequence is a hypothesis to test, not a finding supplied by general cell doubling. Oxytocin proliferation responses depend on the cell model.
- Select the next investigation by biological relevance, accessible measurements and a clear test, rather than whichever visual looks most similar. The present visual exercise does not rank the three alternative directions.
