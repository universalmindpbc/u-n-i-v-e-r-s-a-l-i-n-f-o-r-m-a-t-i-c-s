# Full-text expansion — 23 October 2026

The retained collection increased from 115 to 174 complete articles. Of these, 160 belong to the existing 758-record library and 14 accompany the 23 new Nature Portfolio entries. After those entries are integrated, coverage is 174 / 781 (22.3%). The requested doubling to 230 has not been reached.

## Retrieval and permission

- Original collection: 115 open-license full texts.
- PMC Cloud pass: 9 additional CC BY articles and 44 author manuscripts marked TDM in the official dataset.
- Publisher pass: 6 additional CC BY articles with matching DOI, substantial body content and an explicit article-level permission statement.
- Total: 130 open-license copies and 44 author manuscripts retained separately for text mining.

PMC versions were discovered through the official Cloud manifest. Identity, retraction status, metadata and article-level permissions were checked; file checksums and paragraph provenance were retained. The publisher pass followed Crossref-deposited official locations, checked DOI identity and permission, and excluded abstract-only landing pages. XML bodies or complete PDF pages were retained with their attribution and rights statements. Figures and external supplementary datasets are not represented as separately acquired research assets.

The PMC pass left 45 records for rights review, 31 absent from the supported Cloud dataset, and one with conflicting permission statements. Seven publisher targets were not retained because the content or permission check failed. A free-to-read location is not automatically permission for redistribution or AI-provider upload. NC/ND/unclear licences were not treated as unrestricted licences.

Files remain local under the Informatics research directories `open-fulltext-20261023` and `text-mining-manuscripts-20261023`. No model training, embeddings, AI-provider upload or public full-text redistribution has been performed. Before deploying a retrieval service, apply each article's rights to the intended use and preserve passage-level attribution. TDM manuscripts are not interchangeable with CC BY copies for redistribution.

PubMed is a publication index that links to PMC and publishers. The additional PMC route recovered author manuscripts missed by the original Europe PMC open-access pass. The original audit reported 270 free-full-text locations; that is a discovery count, not a count of downloaded reusable articles. Other publisher and institutional locations remain candidates for subsequent permission-aware retrieval. No authors were contacted and no subscriptions were purchased.
