# Daytab+ research review — 26 October 2026

This review prioritises successful outcomes that can inform Universal Mind's intervention development. The selection includes useful exploratory mechanisms and one reusable genomic dataset. It is a focused discovery review, not a systematic review or a claim that every indexed intervention is effective.

## Result prepared for staging

70 new publication records: 20 Sound, 12 Vision/EMDR and 38 Language/meditation records. The public library grows from 821 to **891 deduplicated publications**. Collection membership is not a count of independent trials: secondary reports and shared cohorts are identified in the records.

Every addition has a verified PubMed identity. The 69 supplied DOIs resolve to matching Crossref records; one paper has no DOI in its PubMed record. Summaries were written from the primary publications' abstracts, with targeted full-text checks for the methylation correction and reusable dataset. Full-text access was obtained for 22 selected papers, but access alone is not a completed risk-of-bias appraisal. No new numerical Jadad score or topic trust score was assigned. Sixty-six additions remain unassessed for Jadad; four nonrandomised studies are marked not applicable.

The same approved research-library paragraph appears in the wiki and both LLM text files, using **“more than 800 publications”**. The exact register count is 891. The About page and homepage are outside this update.

The molecular searches produced 17 distinct source mappings across related categories. Across the full review, **213 distinct Daytab-linked PubMed identities** were resolved. The displayed Daytab counts include overlapping categories and multiple reports from the same trial; they are not quality-weighted counts.

The Daytab intervention search did not expose a dedicated isochronic-tone category. The entrainment search also returned unrelated cardiac mapping procedures. The broader auditory search included auditory stimulation (583 records), low-frequency sound stimulation (22) and nature sounds (37); these category totals were not treated as equivalent to binaural or isochronic research.

Primary-source searches retrieved 515 auditory records, 494 EMDR/randomisation records, 232 mantra/randomisation records, 216 molecular records, and the first 1,000 citation-ranked mindfulness RCT records from a 2,738-hit search. These searches produced 2,422 unique records; direct Daytab links supplied another 28, for **2,450 bibliographic discovery records**. These are candidates, not 2,450 eligible or appraised studies. Forty-three of the 70 selected additions have a direct Daytab source mapping; the remaining 27 emerged from the complementary primary-source searches.

The screen explicitly distinguishes meditation from the unrelated MANTRA eating-disorder acronym, and auditory isochronic stimulation from unrelated uses of “isochronic” in biological and cardiac research.

## Particularly useful findings

- **Matched-tone auditory control:** a 40-person knee-replacement trial reported first-day morphine consumption of 5.75 versus 11.85 mg with 4 Hz binaural stimulation. Pain ratings were similar. This supplies a concrete protocol and an objective medication-use endpoint. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32412916/)
- **Anaesthetic-dose endpoint:** a 69-person trial reported an 18 mg reduction in propofol needed for loss of response after 1 Hz binaural beats. Anxiety and EEG differences were not significant. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40563070/)
- **Active psychotherapy comparison:** the 173-veteran mantram trial used blinded outcome assessment and found greater PTSD improvement than present-centred therapy. Its hyperarousal report is labelled as a secondary analysis of the same cohort. [Main trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29921143/) · [Secondary analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31632617/)
- **EMDR after traumatic birth:** two early postpartum sessions reduced symptom severity in a trial randomising 151 women. Clinician-diagnosed PTSD rates did not differ significantly. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40769315/)
- **Scalable digital mindfulness:** a 2,182-person active-control trial found a small stress benefit; a separate 1,458-person waitlist-controlled trial found a larger reported effect. The difference in comparator matters when interpreting effect sizes. [Active-control trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36006668/) · [Employee trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39808431/)
- **Reusable expression data:** the 60-participant mindful-awareness versus health-education dataset links to **GSE108215**. Its value is access to measured gene-expression data for reanalysis; the data paper alone does not establish treatment success. [Data paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29900257/) · [GEO accession cited in the paper](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE108215)
- **Genetic moderation:** the DRD2 rs6277 finding is an exploratory genotype-by-intervention analysis in breast-cancer survivors. It is not evidence that MBSR changed DRD2 expression. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39413359/)
- **Corrected methylation result:** the FKBP5 paper's 2021 correction reverses the originally stated direction: responders increased methylation and nonresponders decreased it. The adjusted p value is 0.052. The curated record incorporates both points and the lack of a corresponding SLC6A4 result. [Correction](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyt.2021.642245)

These studies offer protocols, comparators, endpoints and biological questions for AMDR development. They do not directly test or validate the Universal Mind/AMDR implementation, passive mantra delivery, OXTR/Fibonacci relationships, or the computer-vision hypothesis.

## Selection and outstanding appraisal

Positive clinical results receive priority. Positive secondary outcomes, within-group improvements and exploratory biological associations are labelled as such. Material limits remain attached to the individual finding rather than being removed to create a stronger impression.

Standalone null findings were not added as a separate expansion theme. They remain visible in the discovery register where relevant to future appraisal. The isochronic-versus-binaural dental trial (PMID 42471303) does not establish superior anxiety reduction, so it was not promoted as a successful isochronic protocol. No newly identified, robust positive isochronic-only RCT was established in this search; existing mechanistic EEG references remain useful context.

Unselected discovery records retain `discovery-candidate-unappraised` status. This does not mean they failed an eligibility review. A full assessment of allocation concealment, attrition, selective reporting, outcome prespecification, conflicts and independent replication remains necessary before assigning high-confidence study-level ratings.

The evidence supports “extensive, clinically searchable research index”. It does not establish a world-ranking or a uniformly high-trust library. Publication, journal and independent-trial counts are distinct.

The public HTML, interactive library and complete JSON/CSV exports are included in the staging release. No production deployment or hosted MCP/assistant corpus mutation is part of this staging update. Git and content hashes record this revision; no new IPFS or DeSci timestamp is claimed here.

Daytab's [method](https://daytab.com/method) explains its abstract-based aggregation and the limits of its outcome counts. The exact primary queries and retrieval bounds are retained in `screening-register.json`.
