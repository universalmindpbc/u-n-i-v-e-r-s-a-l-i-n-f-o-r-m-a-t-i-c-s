# Fibonacci mathematics in computation and biology — source audit

Verified 24 October 2026 (Australia/Sydney). Research-only handoff for Universal Research, General, and Universal One. No website, Git ref, deployment, or approved existing wording was changed.

## Scientific judgement

The collection supports distinct mathematical results, engineered computational uses, and bounded biological observations or models. These are different kinds of evidence. None of the 30 papers tests or validates an OXTR–Fibonacci relationship. No clinical evidence grade is inferred.

The strongest direct biological-computational links are a combinatorial enumeration of defined fatty-acid classes, engineered DNA logic, and a search over bacterial expression patterns. The DNA circuit uses a truth table for the first 16 Fibonacci-word elements; the bacterial study selects subnetworks for agreement with a finite numerical task. Neither establishes a general natural law of genomic computation.

## Source search and scope

The starting source was [Nature search: Fibonacci](https://www.nature.com/search?q=fibonacci&journal=). The captured search inventory contains 200 unique result links from pages 1–4. Attempts at later pages returned no extracted article links, so this is a scoped bibliography rather than a complete census. Focused web searches and reference following supplied biological-patterning papers that the first result pages did not surface. Search records remain in source-cache/.

Inclusion required a primary Nature Portfolio article with substantive Fibonacci, generalized Fibonacci, or golden-angle mathematics in the analysed process, model, device or algorithm. Two plant-hormone papers provide explicitly labelled mechanistic context for phyllotaxis. Articles with only tangential mentions, off-scope game theory, reviews, news and commentary were not used to fill the target.

The Nature Index entry for “Spiral phyllotaxis in the moss Physcomitrium patens emerges from simple division rules of the apical cell” points to Current Biology (10.1016/j.cub.2026.01.059), so it is outside the Nature Portfolio restriction. Nature-hosted indexing does not establish Nature Portfolio publication.

## Boundaries that remain visible in the records

- Plant and algal geometry does not establish a shared molecular cause. The Sargassum results specifically leave the signal unresolved.
- The jellyfish paper uses earlier observations and an assumed optimality criterion; it is not a new controlled evolutionary experiment.
- The dragonfly analysis measures angular distributions. Functional optimality and a developmental cause remain untested in that study.
- The starch paper imposes a golden-angle arrangement in a proposed model. Geometric and scattering agreement does not uniquely determine native structure.
- Lipid enumeration counts allowed structures under stated chemistry constraints; it does not measure biological abundances.
- Classical optical-wave analogues, quantum hardware measurements, mathematical proofs, numerical predictions and proposed cryptographic protocols retain separate study-design labels.
- Statistical image-encryption tests do not establish deployment security. Quantum protocol proposals do not establish a measured communication advantage.
- Quantum proofs of principle do not establish scalable fault tolerance or a general quantum speedup.
- The PLLA branching counts are approximate; the authors describe counting difficulties.
